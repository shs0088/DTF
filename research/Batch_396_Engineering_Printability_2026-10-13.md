# Batch 396 — Geometry and quality engineering

Spanish university material explains morphological reconstruction, skeletonization and distance transforms. Opening can erase isolated small components, closing can fill holes, and erosion can destroy thin strokes. Audit connected components, enclosed counters, Arabic dots, diacritics and minimum stroke width in millimeters before and after processing. Do not silently remove disconnected design details.

Russian area-weighted resampling work shows that scale/rotation order changes interpolation artifacts; interpolation does not recover source information. Turkish segmentation-guided enhancement limits unwanted global histogram equalization effects. Portuguese anisotropic diffusion and Italian variational denoising suggest region-specific enhancement rather than universal smoothing. Quality metrics alone do not establish DTF printability: physical white-underbase, ink/film, ICC and pressed-sample calibration remain required.
