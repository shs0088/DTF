## High-value engineering findings
1. **Critical external code defect — Lanczos center weight.** A Japanese original tutorial's sample `lanczos(x,lobe)` returns zero when `x===0`, whereas the mathematical sinc limit and Lanczos center weight must be 1. Never copy untested tutorial code. Add kernel symmetry, normalization, center-weight and flat-field preservation tests. Nearest-neighbor downsizing aliases; bicubic/Lanczos can ring. Process fractional transparency with explicit premultiplied linear-light conventions, guarded unpremultiplication and adequate low-pass filtering.
2. **Image enhancement is not monotonic quality improvement.** Korean CLAHE example works on a luminance-like channel to avoid independent RGB hue shifts. Russian rank-cluster denoiser uses Otsu-like partition of local pixel samples and compares median, Wiener, bilateral and NLM. Treat vendor/blog performance claims as unvalidated until reproduced on DTF artwork. German USM research shows bright/dark halos and chromatic shifts at high-contrast edges; prefer selective/luminance-only sharpening, evaluated at final print dimensions.
3. **Matting identifiability and layer separation.** Microsoft's blue-screen matting research explains the underdetermined nature of extracting alpha and uncontaminated foreground RGB from a single composite; a second known backing supplies constraints. Chinese recurrent video-matting analysis distinguishes alpha, foreground RGB and segmentation outputs and high-resolution guided refinement, but video recurrent architecture is not a drop-in still-artwork algorithm. FactorMatte separates shadows and interaction effects: mockup shadows are not necessarily printable design content.
4. **Morphology must protect topology.** Spanish morphological interpolation and reconstruction with propagation criteria, and French morphology lecture, support connected-component and shape-aware checks. Protect disconnected Arabic dots/diacritics, punctuation, internal holes and thin strokes before any erosion, opening or underbase choke. Use 4/8-connectivity and minimum physical feature-width QA.
5. **Thresholding and denoising must be judged downstream.** Turkish Otsu and Portuguese original segmentation research distinguish binary intensity partition from contour-based methods. Mexican radar research evaluates speckle denoising before thresholding and morphology; its quantitative metrics and final classification results are application-specific. Evaluate print-design preprocessing on edge fidelity, typography and alpha-gradient preservation rather than smoothness alone.

## Implementation acceptance criteria
- Keep immutable original master and SHA-256 provenance; every mask, processed PNG, white plate and mockup is a separate derivative.
- Explicitly track straight/premultiplied alpha, linear-light vs encoded color, ICC profile and resampling filter.
- Separate alpha geometry correction from foreground RGB decontamination; never globally delete white artwork.
- Before/after gates: connected-component counts, hole counts, narrowest strokes, fractional alpha histogram, edge halo contrast on black/white/garment-color backgrounds.
- Convert choke from mm to output raster pixels and avoid double application in prepress and RIP. No universal choke, white density or LPI without printer calibration.
- Distinguish artistic dithering from RIP halftoning. Preserve print-master geometry while generating mockup derivatives.
