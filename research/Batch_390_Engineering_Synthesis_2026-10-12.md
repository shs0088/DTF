## Engineering findings and implementation implications
1. **Separate three image masks.** Chinese background-replacement and Bayesian-matting analyses explain that a binary semantic mask, an uncertain-region trimap and a continuous alpha matte answer different questions. For each upload, preserve segmentation, trimap, alpha, reconstructed foreground RGB and RGBA derivatives separately. Bayesian matting relies on local foreground/background priors; near alpha=0, inversion for foreground color is ill-conditioned. Sources: https://www.cnblogs.com/21home/p/22352782 and https://www.cnblogs.com/HHCCKs/p/10266742.html.
2. **White underbase is a calibrated print channel, not a PNG cleanup setting.** PrintFactory's DTF guide requires physically pressed LPI and white-opacity charts, and states that white/black alignment should first be corrected mechanically. Its white-offset adjustment occurs at output and is not shown in the RIP preview. Image-processing guidance describes 25–35 LPI as a common evaluation region, with ~0.1–0.2 mm inward choke for some modes; these are starting examples, not universal DTF limits. Source: https://support.printfactory.cloud/portal/en/kb/articles/direct-t and https://support.printfactory.cloud/portal/en/kb/articles/direct-to-film-image-processing.
3. **White ink settings have multiple precedence levels.** PrintFactory PMMs can retain an auto-white step while custom white generation/choke is added elsewhere, risking unintended compound adjustments. DTF Station documents queue print-mode defaults versus individual job adjustments. Record a single effective white generator, choke owner, offset, maximum white and halftone settings per job and surface conflicts in preflight. Sources: https://support.printfactory.cloud/portal/en/kb/articles/direct-t, https://support.printfactory.cloud/portal/en/kb/articles/direct-to-film-image-processing, https://dtfstation.com/knowledge-base/how-to-print-the-right-amount-of-white-with-the-set-maximum-white-ink-feature/, https://dtfstation.com/knowledge-base/how-to-perform-quick-white-ink-adjustments/.
4. **Transparent texture fringes can be introduced during resizing.** Russian implementation examples show premultiplied-alpha filtering and transparent-pixel RGB color propagation as two distinct defenses against fringes. Japanese resampling comparisons demonstrate that sample, area-mixing and filtered resizing differ in antialiasing, edge alignment and blur; another Swift implementation encountered partial-alpha border artifacts from noninteger output extents. Recommendation: linear-light premultiplied RGBA resampling, explicit output geometry and multi-background verification; never modify Ready-to-Print Master. Sources: https://habr.com/ru/companies/playrix/articles/336304/, https://qiita.com/yoya/items/b1590de289b623f18639, https://qiita.com/Kyome/items/5cd1438f889b2a5e200f.
5. **Dithering and screening are not interchangeable.** The Spanish technical article compares Floyd–Steinberg (7/16, 3/16, 5/16, 1/16), serpentine scans, Bayer thresholds and blue-noise masks. Portuguese SAi RIP documentation distinguishes error diffusion, stochastic screens and their computational tradeoffs. Artistic dithering must not be automatically compounded with RIP screening. Sources: https://piclab.click/es/articles/image-dithering/ and https://support.thinksai.com/hc/pt/articles/4405452742932-Propriedades-do-Trabalho-Separador-Gest%C3%A3o-de-Cor.
6. **Color-based removal cannot repair mixed RGB edge contamination.** The German technical page explicitly notes that color tolerance and alpha feathering do not reconstruct foreground RGB where it has already mixed with a former background. Use alpha estimation and foreground-color decontamination as separate QA stages. Source: https://photovoid.com/de/tools/transparent-color/.
7. **Thresholding and morphology must preserve lettering topology.** Arabic course material and executable English lessons cover global/local thresholds, erosion and dilation; a journal abstract compares global histogram and iterative local thresholds. Preflight should compare isolated components, holes, stroke widths, Arabic dots/diacritics and white-on-white artwork before/after operations. Sources: https://cbej.uomustansiriyah.edu.iq/index.php/cbej/article/view/6672, https://mahmoudabas.com/ar/courses/ros2-practical/computer-vision-lab-image-processing-object-detection, https://sbirchfield.github.io/cvintro/lessons/lesson03_thresholding_morphology.html.
8. **Color-space choice changes segmentation geometry.** Turkish k-means research compares RGB, Lab and HSV and reports changes in object counts and pixel quantities. Use color-space-specific evaluation rather than assuming RGB k-means is universally optimal. Source: https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/ecjse/article/1141148.
9. **Upload handling is a separate integrity gate.** Japanese HEIC conversion guidance distinguishes extension, declared MIME and decoded bytes; orientation and format conversion can change downstream image geometry. Validate binary signatures and decoded dimensions, retain the immutable master, and process derivatives asynchronously. Sources: https://qiita.com/nenkajp/items/7735ae2efe9efdc6357a and https://zenn.dev/tkpurine/articles/bg-remover-rembg.
10. **Source strength matters.** Manufacturer/RIP documentation is appropriate for device-workflow behavior; implementation tutorials suggest test cases; academic landing abstracts do not establish full-paper verification. All printer-specific numerical values require physical pressed samples. No physical printer calibration was done in this batch.

## Priority acceptance tests
- White-only CMYK=(0,0,0,0), alpha=1 artwork retains a nonzero intended white channel.
- White choke converted mm-to-pixels using effective output resolution; thin-stroke support flagged rather than silently deleted.
- RGB and alpha channel alignment remains stable through crop, resize and export.
- Alpha histogram and previews against black, white and saturated backgrounds reveal residual background.
- Topology checks flag lost Arabic dots, punctuation, holes and narrow strokes.
- No double-choke, double-screen or duplicate color conversion; print mode and job override precedence explicit.
- Original Ready-to-Print Master hash unchanged by previews, mockups and texture processing.
