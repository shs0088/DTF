# DTF Smart Prepress Batch 440 research summary

Date: 2026-10-15. 29 newly opened and locally distinct sources; provisional cumulative 4,900 of 10,000. The global historical unique count is not certified.

New findings: Palette PNG can preserve multiple alpha values using tRNS, but particular exporters may threshold to binary alpha. Separate alpha estimation from foreground RGB reconstruction. Premultiply before image resampling and use zero-alpha safeguards. Protect Arabic dots, small disconnected components and internal holes during morphological cleanup. Total variation denoising and bilateral filters require defect-specific selection and edge-preservation checks. DTF white-underbase and choke must be calibrated and assigned to one processor, not duplicated by the RIP. Offset-paper ICC profiles are not substitutes for calibrated DTF profiles. Mockup derivatives must not modify approved printing masters.
