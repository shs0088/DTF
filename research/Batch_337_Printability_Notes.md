# Batch 337 printability notes

Distinguish impulse from Gaussian noise before filtering. Protect small text, dots and internal openings. Evaluate segmentation using edge, topology and pixel metrics.

White-underbase choke must use physical print dimensions and printer calibration. At 300 effective PPI, 0.15 mm is about 1.77 pixels per side. Do not apply offset AM/FM screen parameters directly to DTF printers. Preserve full alpha during PNG export and resizing.
