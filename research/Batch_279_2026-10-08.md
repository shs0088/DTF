## Engineering synthesis
- Run morphological feature-width, connected-component, hole and intentional-speckle QA before/after cleaning; threshold only QA masks when needed. Convert kernel sizes to final physical millimeters.
- Use GrabCut/watershed/floodFill as segmentation or trimap proposals, then a separate matting and RGB foreground-color stage. Never equate categorical probable foreground with soft opacity.
- For border flood-fill, protect interior white, record connectivity/tolerances, test leaks through small antialiased gaps; never use arbitrary alpha offsets.
- Store a halftone manifest with threshold matrix or exact diffusion coefficients, scan order, output physical size/DPI, color working space, dot gain/loss calibration and RIP/PrintMode ownership. Verify coverage and no unintended small dots after transfer.
- PSNR/SSIM of denoising must use clean ground truth; if absent, use no-reference indicators and independent topology/edge retention. Source-vs-derivative MSE is only a change metric.
- Guard low-alpha unpremultiplication and preserve a separate mockup-safe derivative; test on white, black and garment colors.
- Do not bake white underbase, choke, CMYK or RIP screening into the master when owned by RIP/PrintMode.
