# Batch 302 Synthesis B — Morphology, Underbase, Mockups
2026-10-09. Research only.

Portuguese boundary formulas (Ledger 302 source 12) distinguish inner A-erode(A) and outer dilate(A)-A mask zones. White-underbase choke is a separate RIP/printer separation, never an edit to the approved print master. A stroke of width w can lose white support when symmetric choke c satisfies w <= 2c; actual settings require physical calibration.

French INRAE reconstruction (source 13) propagates marker seeds under a mask. Disconnected components without markers disappear. Protect Arabic dots, diacritics, holes, tiny text and isolated decorative shapes.

Russian texture-filtering analysis (source 8) demonstrates RGB leakage from fully transparent texels during bilinear scaling. Apply premultiplied filtering or edge-color bleed to derived mockup textures only.

Test alpha error, foreground RGB, connected-component topology, physical stroke width, white support, halftone dot survival and actual transfer results independently. No universal DTF print threshold was established.

The cumulative 2547 remains provisional, not globally certified. No merge, deployment, storefront or print-master modification.
