## Engineering synthesis / acceptance tests
- **Background-removal order is critical:** for solid-color art, detect border-connected background before any narrow mask dilation; protect disconnected same-color foreground details. Assert preservation of interior white letters, holes, highlights and distress dots. Test broken outlines for flood leakage.
- **Alpha/RGB integrity:** separate alpha geometry, foreground-RGB decontamination, and texture sampling. Never use repeated browser Canvas round-trips as the sole source of canonical master RGBA; low alpha can quantize RGB irreversibly. Test synthetic RGBA for alpha={0,1,2,10,128,255}, on black/white/garment backgrounds.
- **Correct compositing math:** tag each working buffer as straight or premultiplied, record color transfer function (linear vs encoded), and ensure conversion exactly once at each boundary. Use premultiplied linear-light values for filtering/compositing when appropriate; export a correctly encoded straight-alpha PNG master.
- **Guided alpha refinement:** high-resolution RGB guidance can preserve structure when upsampling coarse alpha; it is *not* restoration of original image detail. Record matte solver resolution separately from image/source PPI.
- **Topology-aware morphology:** kernels expressed in millimetres at locked final size; opening may erase intended tiny art, closing may seal counters. Record connected-component and hole counts before/after; require user-visible undo/review for ambiguous regions.
- **Defringe is not choke:** RGB color contamination must be fixed in the image stage; white-underbase choke belongs to calibrated RIP/PrintMode and must not silently remove thin-feature white support. Keep preview/mockup derivative distinct from print master.
- **ICC and render-output contracts:** verify embedded profile and alpha representation on import/export; do not interpret DPI metadata as newly created pixels. White underbase and LPI values remain device-specific calibration.
- **QA test set:** solid-background cutout with enclosed white, hair/glass soft alpha, distressed punctuation, low-alpha red edge, transparent RGB padding, mipmapped mockup, two physical print sizes and two RIP print modes.
