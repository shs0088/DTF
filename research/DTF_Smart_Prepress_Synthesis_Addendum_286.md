# DTF Smart Prepress Synthesis Addendum 286

Weak-alpha residues require histogram and multi-background QA. Canvas may change low-alpha RGB and wide-gamut colors. Morphological cleanup must preserve holes, dots, and thin strokes. Use fractional-alpha compositing rather than binary masks. Treat RIP white-underbase and choke as printer-calibrated processes. Keep print masters immutable and mockup derivatives separate.

Research only; no deployment or merge.
