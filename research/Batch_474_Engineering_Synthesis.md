## Findings
1. **White-layer generation is separate from choke.** PrintFactory exposes modes such as Shape, Flood, Under Color, Under White, Transparency, Density, inverse density and Fill-up. Verify intended white-channel semantics before shrinking the edge.
2. **Choke is a calibration variable, not a universal pixel constant.** Use small controlled increments and physical test transfers. Tie units to the RIP configuration; protect small type and fine strokes.
3. **Alpha and foreground RGB require separate QA.** Candidate-color confidence and a sparsity prior can reduce blurry matte artifacts, but edge RGB may still be contaminated even when alpha looks plausible. Composite over black, white and saturated backgrounds.
4. **Otsu-style thresholding has alternatives worth testing.** A Korean study proposes within-class standard deviation instead of variance to reduce class-distribution bias. Treat it as a candidate until tested on representative artwork.
5. **Multilevel threshold search can become costly.** The Turkish CHIO paper reports promising comparisons on only six images. Benchmark time, repeatability and mask quality on a larger dataset before production use.
6. **Localized enhancement may avoid global histogram damage.** Active-contour segmentation followed by region-specific equalization is a candidate pattern; compare color shifts, edge artifacts and detail preservation against an untouched source.
7. **An edge detector is not a matte.** Similarity-based boundary proposals can help initialize segmentation, but they do not estimate soft alpha or decontaminate foreground RGB. Keep these gates separate.
8. **Mockup-safe composites need color adaptation.** Saturation-aware chromatic adaptation can reduce mismatches between subject and replacement-scene illuminants. Keep preview-only adaptation separate from the print master.
9. **Iterative cleanup risks erasing weak strokes.** DeepOtsu learns a normalized image and then thresholds it. Any transfer to logos or distressed artwork must preserve faint lettering, dots and outlines.
10. **Threshold stability is a robustness test.** Perturb thresholds slightly and measure changes in IoU, boundary displacement, connected components and minimum stroke survival.

## Recommended gates
- Preserve original RGB, alpha and ICC profile.
- Record whether white underbase is generated by prepress or the RIP.
- Verify white-generation mode before choke adjustment; assign choke to one stage only.
- Test thin text, counters, narrow strokes and gradients.
- Compare masks against reference data using IoU plus boundary/topology metrics.
- Evaluate alpha and edge RGB separately on contrasting backgrounds.
- Keep mockup color adaptation separate from the print master.
- Log the effective RIP mode and job-level overrides.
- Require physical test transfers before adopting vendor-suggested white/choke values.
