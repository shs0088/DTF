# Batch 311 — Prepress and Corpus Caveats

Fifteen locally distinct pages were read, including original Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, Spanish, German, Italian, Turkish and Indonesian work. Previous provisional upper bound 2,672; new upper bound 2,687. Global semantic deduplication is not yet complete.

Segmentation is not matting. Border flood fill may preserve enclosed white foreground but fails when foreground is connected to similarly colored background. Keep Arabic diacritics, holes and fine strokes intact. Compare RGB and Alpha changes independently, and validate denoising at final physical dimensions.

A German textile source describes partial-Alpha behavior with a white underbase in DTG. Do not assume its settings apply to DTF. Verify RIP, film, powder, cure and fabric by print tests. Keep mockup textures and dither previews separate from the immutable Ready-to-Print Master.

Previously counted ZH, KO, FR and EN pages were excluded. Inaccessible Portuguese pages were not counted. Source ledger segments A, B, C1 and C2b are in docs; complete local ledger includes remaining entries.
