## Engineering findings

1. **Linear-light and premultiplied alpha are separate requirements.** Japanese resampling and Go image/color studies, Chris Lomont's color-math analysis, and official OpenSlide ARGB documentation require explicit color-space and alpha-mode contracts. For physically correct compositing, decode ICC/sRGB into linear floating point, premultiply RGB by linear alpha, filter/composite, safely unpremultiply, then encode to the intended color profile and straight-alpha PNG. A 50% linear-light mix of black and white encodes to ~0.735 sRGB, not 0.5. Intentional artistic gradients may use other spaces.
2. **Foreground RGB decontamination is distinct from alpha erosion.** Reopened previously counted Chinese measurements show a halo-removal option can remove light foreground regions by altering alpha. Original German color-to-alpha guidance also demonstrates the risk of deleting background-colored artwork. Preserve legitimate white letters, Arabic dots, translucent effects, and internal holes. Inspect RGB and alpha independently against black/white/garment backgrounds.
3. **Segmentation needs content-specific topology gates.** Spanish CIELAB region tracking and adaptive morphological descriptors, Portuguese adaptive active contours and shape-aware region merging provide alternative algorithms, not universal winners. Compare connected components, holes, minimum stroke width, and boundary displacement before and after processing. Their seismic, agricultural, medical, and remote-sensing results do not establish DTF print quality.
4. **Denoising/edge metrics cannot be reduced to PSNR.** Arabic comparisons of mean, median, Wiener and LPG-PCA denoisers and of edge detectors use PSNR/MSE/RMSE/AIC. Smart Prepress also needs effective PPI at physical print size, topology, edge overshoot, alpha fringe, and typography readability. Avoid unsubstantiated claims that upscaling recovers genuine detail.
5. **Antialiasing and RIP screening must be separate.** Italian antialiasing research explains why white-matted antialiased pixels halo on black and why binary GIF transparency differs from fractional PNG alpha. Do not flatten/dither the immutable printing master. Calibrated RIP halftoning belongs in the printer-specific output workflow.
6. **White-underbase choke needs geometry and RIP-mode verification.** Prior supplier guides reopened in this run conflict about whether the white or CMYK silhouette is reduced. Ordinary white-underbase geometry generally requires white inset relative to color; verify the actual RIP parameter using a two-layer test. Prevent file-level plus RIP double-choke. At 300 effective PPI, 0.15 mm is ~1.77 px per side, so a 0.20 mm isolated line may lose white support. This is math, not a universal printer preset.
7. **Separate master and derivative processing.** The Japanese automated sticker CLI separates image processing, validation and packaging; OpenSlide and Transduce documentation demonstrate alpha-mode interoperability. Preserve immutable original and explicitly selected Ready-to-Print Master; generate previews/mockups separately; reopen exported PNG and check ICC, alpha, dimensions, geometry, and hashes. Do not reuse sticker dimensions as DTF limits.
