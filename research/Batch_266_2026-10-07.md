## Engineering synthesis updates
### 1. Separate geometry from density and screening
White-underbase geometry (mask/choke/spread), white density, and screening method must remain independent parameters. Changing one must not silently mutate the others.

### 2. Add fault-domain diagnosis
A visible white-edge or weak-white defect must be classified across:
- artwork alpha/RGB edge contamination,
- RIP underbase/choke/spread,
- mechanical registration/film feed,
- white-ink circulation/nozzle health,
- ink-limit/profile,
- powder/cure/transfer.
This avoids falsely repairing artwork when the fault is physical.

### 3. Treat RIP as operation owner
Operation ownership should include ICC, white generation, choke/spread, screening, ink limits, channel mapping, print order/device rasterization. Smart Prepress should prevent duplicate ownership.

### 4. Calibration, not universal constants
White density and choke values must be profile/calibration parameters tied to printer, ink, film, powder, cure, garment and print mode. Vendor starting points are evidence, not standards.

### 5. Controlled test-sheet protocol
Calibration sheets should keep artwork/film/cure constant while varying one parameter at a time, and include solids, skin/photo tones, small text, thin lines, gradients, large solids, white-on-colour and fine transparent details.
