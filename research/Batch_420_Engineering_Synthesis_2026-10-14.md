## Key findings and engineering requirements
1. **White underbase/RIP:** PrintFactory documentation separates Alpha, Shape, Under Color, Density and Spot methods, minimum-alpha cutoff, named spot mapping and multiple white layers. White generation can occur at RIP output resolution. Vendor examples cite approximately 25–35 LPI test screening and 0.1–0.15 mm inward choke; neither is universal. White-max, LPI and offset must be physically calibrated with printer/ink/film/powder/garment. Prevent double-choke between prepress and RIP; keep millimeters and plate provenance explicit.
2. **Alpha quantization:** A Japanese controlled PNG-8 exporter test converted 256 alpha levels to 2, destroying soft shadows. This is specific to the exporter, not a PNG-8 format limitation: palette PNG may use multi-level tRNS alpha. Reopen outputs, compare alpha histograms, and composite on black, white and garment colors.
3. **Trimap/alpha separation:** Chinese AdaMatting analysis separates trimap-adaptation classification from fractional alpha regression. Another Chinese tutorial uses erosion/dilation to construct an unknown trimap band but contains undefined alpha_np and placeholders; do not copy untested examples. Preserve known-foreground dots, diacritics and narrow strokes.
4. **Morphology:** Korean tutorial and original French morphology textbook show erosion removes small components. Geodesic reconstruction can recover surviving marker shapes but cannot recreate components absent from the marker. Record component count, holes, min stroke width, 4/8 connectivity and protected glyphs before/after cleanup.
5. **Dither vs RIP screening:** Original Spanish implementation guides compare Floyd–Steinberg, Atkinson, Bayer, wider kernels and serpentine traversal. Turkish OCR research shows preprocessing quality is task-dependent. Keep artistic dithering independent from RIP halftoning and check minimum printable dot/white support.
6. **Color and mockup QA:** Italian ICC research describes source/destination profile conversion and rendering intents. Portuguese registration work describes interpolation under geometric transformations. Use separate mockup derivatives, preserve immutable print masters and source ICC profiles; do not treat resampling as restored detail.
7. **Implementation testing:** Japanese Canvas diff tutorial motivates per-pixel QA. The deblotch Python package documents median/mean-shift flat-art cleanup, alpha-capable output constraints, OpenCV metadata loss, and golden-image regression testing. Track ICC/EXIF explicitly and test exported files, not previews alone.
