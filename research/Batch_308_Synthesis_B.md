# DTF Smart Prepress — Batch 308 synthesis B
## Noise, topology and restoration
Arabic research separates impulse-noise detection from value estimation. Portuguese morphology and Turkish comparisons show that cleanup settings are image-specific. Require before/after connected-component and hole checks; preserve Arabic dots, diacritics, tiny counters, white lettering and isolated artwork. Record changed pixels and provide rollback.

Italian sources cover Richardson–Lucy, Van Cittert, total variation and Perona–Malik. Sharpening/deconvolution can amplify noise or thicken strokes; evaluate at final print size with edge overshoot, effective PPI, topology, color drift and readable text. A paper's runtime result does not prove throughput on large DTF images.

## Output and validation
Maintain separate immutable original, reversible working copy, approved Ready-to-Print Master and mockup texture. Test 1–2px lines, low-alpha colored edges, border-touching objects, non-square resize, filter-dependent edge bleed, salt-and-pepper/Gaussian/JPEG noise, halftone gradients and ICC round trips. DTF white-underbase choke, minimum dot survival and transfer durability require actual RIP/printer calibration; no universal numeric tolerance was established.
