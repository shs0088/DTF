## Engineering findings
- Add a deterministic fast path for near-uniform backgrounds: perceptual border-colour estimation, edge-connected flood fill, edge-band alpha estimation, then foreground-RGB decontamination.
- When the same render is available over two sufficiently different known backgrounds, differential compositing can recover alpha and foreground RGB; exact alignment and denominator stability are required.
- Inspect Result, Alpha and Edge-band representations separately and retain manual alpha edits as provenance.
- Treat screened fade tails as physical structures: measure isolated-component density, minimum dot/bridge size, white-support survival and adhesive-support survival at final size.
- Diagnose halos by domain before repair: foreground RGB contamination, fractional-alpha eligibility, underbase/choke geometry, registration, or downstream process.
- Image-processing APIs need decoded-megapixel limits in addition to byte limits, plus file-count, concurrency and rate limits; untrusted image processing should be isolated.
