Engineering findings:
- Use border-connected flood-fill for flat backgrounds; global removal of white destroys white foreground and internal details.
- Alpha feathering is not foreground RGB decontamination; inspect composite against black, white and saturated backgrounds.
- Preserve topology of letters, isolated dots, holes and small art components when applying erosion, dilation or denoising.
- Multiscale morphology and adaptive thresholding are scene-dependent, not universally safe DTF artwork enhancement.
- A stroke of width w pixels may lose all white support after choke c pixels per side if w <= 2c. This is a geometric preflight heuristic, not a universal printer setting.
- On-device WebGPU matting may improve throughput but requires fallback and license review; one Korean engineering report measured substantial speedups on its own hardware.
- Physical printability and RIP white-plane behavior require calibration prints at the final size.
