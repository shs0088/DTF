## Synthesis delta
- Add a low-cost deterministic route for simple solid/near-solid backgrounds: border/corner sampling -> color-distance/tolerance mask -> connectivity/flood-fill protection -> edge refinement -> RGB decontamination -> multi-background composite QA.
- Keep tolerance, alpha/edge smoothing, denoise, and foreground-RGB decontamination as separate provenance-bearing operations. They solve different failure modes and must not be collapsed into a single "clean edges" control.
- Multi-background preview is not cosmetic. At minimum test checkerboard + white + black; for DTF extend to representative garment colors because residual fringe can disappear on one background and become obvious on another.
- Local/browser execution can improve privacy and latency, but it does not confer print authority. Final-resolution effective-PPI, alpha, fringe, minimum-feature, and underbase QA still apply.
