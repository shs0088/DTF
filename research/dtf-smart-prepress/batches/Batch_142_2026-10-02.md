## Engineering synthesis
### 1. White generation is a multi-parameter transform
RDX and Flexi evidence reinforces that white generation must not be represented by one “underbase” switch. Provenance should separate source authority (alpha vs CMYK/color-derived), threshold, density, choke/bleed, blur/edge softness and screening.

### 2. Ink limiting belongs to the calibrated output condition
RDX explicitly separates per-channel CMYK limits from total ink limit and couples them with monotonic spline calibration and spectrophotometer/CGATS profiling. Smart Prepress should model ink limiting as part of OutputConditionProfile rather than mutate the artwork master.

### 3. Halftone algorithm identity is insufficient
Threshold, Bayer ordered dither, Floyd-Steinberg and Stucki have different spatial error/noise structures. ScreenArtifact therefore needs algorithm/kernel or matrix, working geometry, quantization/drop mode, and calibrated output condition.

### 4. Binary alpha cleanup is destructive and must be routed narrowly
TheToolApp provides a concrete implementation that snaps alpha around a fixed threshold. This can remove faint fringe on opaque logos but necessarily destroys legitimate fractional transparency. Smart Prepress should route binary alpha snapping only to artwork classified as hard-edge/opaque and retain the pre-clean matte for rollback and QA.

### 5. RIP capability and printer output resolution remain separate from artwork authority
RDX exposes printer pass/output modes up to high device DPI while independently performing auto-DPI and dimension checks. Device DPI must never be substituted for effective artwork PPI at final physical size.
