## Engineering synthesis

- Mathematical morphology is geometry-authoritative: structuring-element shape, orientation and physical radius must be provenance. Erosion can destroy thin strokes; closing can incorrectly join nearby components.
- Opening/closing should be purpose-routed (speck removal, hole repair, mask repair, choke/spread) rather than exposed as one generic strength slider. Topology/minimum-stroke QA is mandatory before/after.
- Downsampling and upsampling require separate policies. Area/box-style resampling is appropriate for reduction; higher-order interpolation or reconstruction has different risks for enlargement.
- Neural super-resolution may create visually plausible but unsupported detail. Record reconstruction authority as deterministic vs generative and rerun glyph/topology/edge/minimum-feature QA after generative SR.
- Screening family and tone/dot-gain compensation are independent authorities. Threshold, patterned dither, error diffusion/FM and halftone geometry must not be silently changed by a tone adjustment.
- Noise classification should precede denoising; median filtering is particularly appropriate for impulse/salt-and-pepper noise and should not be replaced by a universal Gaussian default.
