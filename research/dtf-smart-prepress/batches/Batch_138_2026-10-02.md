## Engineering synthesis
- **Halftone authority must be output-condition aware.** Screening is not just an image effect. Store screening family (AM/FM/diffusion), LPI/frequency, angle/scan behavior, dot shape, tonal preconditioning, dot-gain compensation, min/max printable dot, output DPI and final physical geometry. Any later resize invalidates screen calibration.
- **Minimum printable tone/dot must be measured, not hard-coded.** The Brandum calibration-wedge model is the correct architecture: derive min-dot, max-dot and dot-gain from the actual press/ink/media condition and bind those measurements to an OutputCondition profile.
- **White-underbase generation is its own artifact.** Choke, white density, black suppression and thin-line protection must be versioned independently from RGB/CMYK artwork and alpha. A choke beyond the measured registration need can erase thin features, so minimum-feature guards are mandatory.
- **Garment-color composites are QA views, not master edits.** Light/dark/multiple garment previews should reveal halos and white-underbase leakage without baking garment simulation into the print master.
- **DTF gradient QA needs a floor policy.** Very low-opacity/low-tone regions can become isolated visible dots, especially when each color dot carries white underneath. Flag long fades that cross the measured printable floor; do not silently clip without user-visible evidence.
- **Color provenance must distinguish input interpretation from output conversion.** Untagged files become an explicit UNMANAGED state. Output ICC is bound to printer/ink/film/screen condition. Physical proof remains the authority for critical brand color.
- **Background removal and privacy are separable concerns.** Local/browser processing can satisfy privacy requirements, but alpha quality still requires edge/halo/fractional-alpha QA at final master geometry.
- **Final-size preflight is mandatory.** 300 ppi metadata alone is insufficient; effective PPI must be computed from pixel dimensions and intended physical print size. Edge/halo and minimum-feature checks must run after the final geometry is fixed.
