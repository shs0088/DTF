## Engineering synthesis

- Morphological boundary conditions are part of reproducibility. PixelSpectra explicitly demonstrates asymmetric neutral padding conventions for erosion/dilation and duality; border policy therefore belongs in morphology provenance, not just kernel shape/size.
- Opening and closing have distinct topology risks: opening removes small bright components; closing fills small dark holes and can join nearby pieces. DTF mask repair must quantify component-count, hole-count and minimum-stroke changes before acceptance.
- Dither working resolution is an algorithm parameter. Newtil exposes pixel-size/downsampling independently from Floyd–Steinberg, Atkinson and Bayer families; screening calibration is invalid after later resize.
- Choke is registration compensation, not a generic edge cleaner. The Spanish DTF source ties visible white borders to registration shift and semitransparent antialiasing, while warning that excessive contraction destroys fine lines. Store choke in physical units and gate it against minimum-feature width.
- DTF RIP architecture should keep white density and white geometry independent. Master Tek describes automatic underbase generation and density control separately from choke, reinforcing distinct WhiteArtifact controls.
- Background-removal routing should retain a coarse semantic mask followed by fractional-alpha matting for hair, glass, shadows and other mixed pixels. Binary segmentation alone cannot represent these boundaries; matting latency can be routed selectively by artwork class.
