## Engineering synthesis delta

### Matting authority
Use:
Segmentation/TrimapArtifact -> MatteArtifact -> ForegroundEstimationArtifact -> CompositeQA.
Do not assume RGB*alpha is a clean extracted foreground. Store trimap polarity, unknown-region geometry, model/solver identity and foreground-estimation method.

### Halo/decontamination
Halo control is not only alpha refinement. Foreground RGB estimation/decontamination must remain a separately inspectable transform. QA should composite on light, dark and chromatic backgrounds.

### Dithering/screening
Tone preservation alone is insufficient. ScreenArtifact should retain algorithm/kernel, traversal policy, random seed where applicable, working geometry, and spatial diagnostics. DTF validation should additionally test minimum printable dot/gap and topology against the calibrated output condition.

### Quantization precision
Dithering may reduce visible banding when reducing precision, but production text/line art and masks need separate policy because added spatial noise can damage edge authority and minimum-feature survival.
