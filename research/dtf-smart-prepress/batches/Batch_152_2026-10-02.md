## Engineering synthesis
- White-channel representation is an interoperability contract. Named W1 spot-channel TIFF can be materially different from a flattened transparency-only export; preserve WhiteOwner and export-channel semantics.
- Choke must be physical-size aware. SR DTF explicitly computes against effective print geometry rather than trusting PNG DPI metadata and combines morphology with edge cleanup and minimum-feature risk.
- Edge cleanup has at least three separable concerns: weak/ghost alpha removal, foreground-RGB decontamination, and controlled anti-alias reconstruction. Do not collapse them into one destructive threshold.
- White generation, white density, choke/spread, screening and ICC/output-condition transforms remain separate authorities. Detect and warn on double ownership between file-side preparation and RIP-side generation.
- Production calibration should use controlled test charts/ranges rather than a universal choke constant. Fine text, large fills and gradient/fractional-alpha regions should be represented in the chart.
- Underbase shrink can erase fine features. Re-run minimum-stroke/component/island survival after white-mask generation and choke at final physical size.
- Vendor numeric presets are calibration priors only, not standards; bind them to printer/ink/film/RIP/output-condition evidence.
