## Synthesis update

- Background removal should remain a staged artifact pipeline: background hypothesis/coarse segmentation -> alpha construction/refinement -> edge treatment -> optional foreground-color decontamination -> export.
- Tolerance, edge softness/feathering, denoise, segmentation model/mode and any border-sampling heuristic belong in provenance because each can change printable edge geometry.
- Denoising routing should classify at least luminance noise, chroma noise, impulse/grain-like noise and compression artifacts before selecting a filter/model. Final QA must check thin strokes, glyph topology, alpha edges and color drift.
- Upload safety must apply limits at multiple stages: compressed bytes before decode; dimensions/megapixels after header/decode validation; decoded/working-buffer budget during processing; output budget on export.
- Preview convenience must never become print authority: final alpha/edge/minimum-feature QA runs at final master geometry.
