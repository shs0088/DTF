## Engineering synthesis
1. Split production white controls into WhiteGeometry/Eligibility, WhiteDensity and ColorManagement. A RIP may expose all three, but they solve different physical problems and must not share one slider or authority.
2. Add ScreeningProvenance: family/dot shape, angle(s), physical output width, target DPI, garment/background assumption, tone curve and white-layer coupling. Any later resize invalidates screen calibration.
3. Add MockupAuthority = PREVIEW_ONLY. Mockup rendering can use garment color and screened appearance but must never silently replace the transparent print master or RIP-ready white separation.
4. Learned denoising needs Model/Scene/Strength provenance and generative-feature flags. Face recovery or reconstructed texture is not neutral cleanup and requires opt-in plus topology/glyph/texture QA.
5. Keep deterministic denoise baselines: bilateral for continuous edge-sensitive noise, median for impulse noise, Gaussian only when intentional blur is acceptable. Compare removed-detail residuals before accepting a learned result.
6. Sharpen only after final geometry. Store radius/amount/threshold and evaluate overshoot halos, noise amplification and chroma shift. Where appropriate, luminance/value-only sharpening can reduce color artifacts, but still requires edge QA.
