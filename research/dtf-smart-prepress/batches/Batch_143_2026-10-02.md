## Engineering synthesis

### 1. Underbase authority must be alpha-aware
For opaque artwork, morphology-based inward choke is reasonable. For fractional-alpha gradients, smoke, hair, glow or feathered edges, binary/nonzero-alpha white generation can destroy intended translucency. Route these assets to an alpha-aware white policy and composite QA.

### 2. Adaptive choke should be constrained by topology
A safe choke cannot exceed what the thinnest printable feature can survive. Model per-design choke from final physical size, minimum feature width and edge complexity, then validate topology after erosion. Do not apply one global choke to a mixed gang sheet.

### 3. Separate white-generation semantics from white density
White mask construction, choke/spread, white density/opacity and white screening are independent operations. Preserve them separately in provenance and in OutputConditionProfile.

### 4. Screening parameters require physical calibration
Dot size/LPI, angle and density are screening parameters, but survival depends on the actual printer/ink/film/RIP/transfer condition. Full-size preview is useful but cannot replace physical calibration/test strips.

### 5. RIP output condition is multi-dimensional
ICC/profile, output DPI, pass count, droplet behavior, per-channel/white density and choke must not be conflated with artwork quality. Printer DPI is not artwork effective PPI.
