## Engineering synthesis

- DTF white-underbase diagnosis must separate geometry/choke, density/opacity, jetting/viscosity, registration, and artwork-alpha causes. Do not treat every visible white defect as a choke problem.
- White generation needs explicit source authority: alpha-derived, spot mask, object coverage, or manual mask. Fractional artwork alpha can legitimately produce weaker white even when printer mechanics are healthy.
- Screening provenance must include working resolution/pixel size. Floyd–Steinberg, Atkinson and Bayer at different grids are materially different output conditions.
- QA for binary/halftone output requires both 1:1 raster inspection and final physical-size simulation; browser-fit previews can visually average dots back into gray.
- Color-management architecture must separate soft-proof profile from actual output-conversion profile, including rendering intent and black-point compensation.
