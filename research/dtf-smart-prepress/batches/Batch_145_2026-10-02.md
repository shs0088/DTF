## Engineering synthesis
- Treat model selection as routing metadata: subject class/model/version/execution backend must be recorded.
- Manual erase/repair is a separate override artifact, not destructive replacement of automatic alpha.
- Edge smoothing/antialias is not equivalent to foreground RGB decontamination; preserve separate alpha-refinement and RGB-decontamination stages.
- Batch/concurrency changes scheduling only; each source image remains independently provenance-tracked and QA-gated.
- Halftoning for DTF belongs to an output/RIP condition; artwork effective PPI is evaluated at final physical size and is independent of printer output DPI.
- A transparent PNG is only a container requirement, not proof of matte quality or printability.

No deployment, merge, Oracle execution, or protected-storefront modification.
