## Engineering synthesis
- Treat segmentation, trimap construction, alpha estimation, foreground-RGB recovery/decontamination, manual alpha correction, and export as separate versioned artifacts.
- For two-background renders, difference matting can recover alpha and foreground color more directly than single-background color-distance masking, but requires pixel-aligned equal geometry and known background colors.
- Trimap erosion/dilation width is a semantic parameter. Wider unknown bands increase matting scope/cost; narrow bands risk excluding mixed pixels. Record both widths and final physical geometry.
- Mask polarity is part of provenance. External-mask inversion mistakes can catastrophically swap foreground/background; validate polarity using coverage and border/background checks before matting.
- Edge decontamination is distinct from alpha refinement: alpha may be geometrically correct while RGB edge pixels retain old-background spill.
- Matting QA should include multiple composite backgrounds (at least light/dark/neutral) because halos invisible on one background can become obvious on another.
- Long-range context matters for broad unknown regions; routing should distinguish thin boundary bands from large ambiguous/translucent regions rather than forcing one matting model.
- Manual alpha brush/eraser edits should be stored as replayable overrides over the automatic matte, not destructively baked into the only master.
- Upload/runtime safety needs independent compressed-byte, decoded-megapixel/dimension, concurrency, rate, memory/work and output-size budgets.
