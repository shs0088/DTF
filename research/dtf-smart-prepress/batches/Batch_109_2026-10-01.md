## Engineering synthesis
1. Add boundary-specific QA: region IoU can look strong while edge MAE remains poor. DTF cutout acceptance should score uncertain-boundary pixels separately, especially thin strands, holes and anti-aliased edges.
2. Make refinement routed by content: clean logos/products may stop after segmentation + guided refinement, while hair/fur/glass/soft fabric edges require fractional-alpha matting and foreground-RGB decontamination.
3. Add AlphaOccupancy diagnostics (transparent / semi-transparent / opaque fractions, extrema and optionally histogram). A sudden loss of semi-transparent population after an operation is a useful regression signal; binary thresholding must be explicit.
4. Keep WhiteAuthority and RIP behavior explicit. Visible artwork white, generated technical underbase, choke and physical white laydown are different concepts. File-side preflight validates geometry; device/RIP calibration validates ink behavior.
5. Convert printability gates to physical units after final geometry. Choke, minimum stroke/hole/island width and halftone dot diameter should be measured in mm; any later resize invalidates the calibration.
6. Halftone/tone floors are profile-specific empirical evidence, not universal constants. Store printer/RIP/film/ink/profile provenance with calibrated minimum useful tone and dot-size thresholds.
