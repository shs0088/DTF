## Engineering synthesis
- White-underbase generation must be modeled as a first-class artifact derived from alpha, not as an implicit RIP side effect. Record alpha threshold, choke in physical units, white amount/density, screening method, and output condition.
- Choke must be evaluated in physical geometry. Pixel-only choke is unsafe because resizing changes its physical width. Compute effective PPI from final print dimensions and convert choke to mm/px only at the final geometry.
- Choke has a topology cost: thin strokes, small text, isolated dots and distressed texture can lose all white support when erosion from opposite edges meets. Run minimum-feature and component survival QA after underbase generation.
- Edge cleanup and white generation are separate authorities. Low-alpha ghost pixels, anti-aliased fringe and background-color contamination can each create dusty/colored halos. Preserve an explicit edge-clean/decontamination artifact before deriving underbase.
- Gradual alpha and binary underbase need separate compatibility paths. Legacy RIPs may require 0/255 masks; modern workflows can preserve graded alpha. Never silently binarize a soft/translucent edge.
- White itself may be halftoned (ordered, error-diffusion or stochastic) for soft-hand/gradient behavior. Screening parameters belong to the white artifact and must be regenerated after any resize.
- RIP/export interoperability requires explicit channel semantics: extra TIFF channels, PSD/PSB spot channels, separate mask PNG, or named spot separation are not interchangeable without validation.
- French Floyd–Steinberg material reinforces that error diffusion conserves quantization error spatially; this is useful for tonal appearance but does not establish physical printability. Minimum printable dot/gap must still be calibrated to the real output condition.
- Indonesian UV-DTF guidance reinforces that underbase generation mode depends on artwork semantics (solid colored pixels versus authored spot channel) and that choke/spread are opposite operations. Do not expose them as a single signed slider without clear semantics.
