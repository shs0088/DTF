## Engineering synthesis

- Choke/decrease is only a geometric white-mask contraction. The Turkish DTF source shows that it can suppress small opaque-edge white protrusion but is insufficient for semitransparent gradients: placing equal white under every nonzero-alpha pixel can destroy intended translucency. Smart Prepress therefore needs an alpha/coverage-aware white-generation policy in addition to choke.
- White generation mode and white density are separate authorities. A RIP may choose binary/nontransparent coverage or tone-dependent gradient white, while density scales deposited white more globally. Preserve these as independent WhiteArtifact parameters.
- Generic error diffusion for printing should operate on a target palette with vector quantization error rather than blindly dithering RGB channels independently. The CNRS exercise explicitly generalizes Floyd–Steinberg to an arbitrary print palette and notes that the color-distance metric itself is a design choice. Add PaletteAuthority and ColorDistanceMetric to screening provenance.
- Dithering can preserve alpha while changing RGB only. This is valuable for mockup-safe assets, but DTF production must separately decide whether white-underbase screening derives from alpha, color coverage, or a dedicated white mask; RGB dither must never silently mutate alpha authority.
- Upload/resource safety belongs in image-processing design: the Korean browser implementation caps bytes, megapixels, side length and output pixels. Adopt independent pre-decode byte limits, post-decode geometry limits, and output/work-buffer budgets.
- Deterministic resampling and generative reconstruction remain different authority classes. Lanczos enlargement does not create semantically new detail; neural SR can. Record ReconstructionAuthority and require stronger QA/review for generated detail.
- Preview/export parity must be explicit. Wimager caps live preview for speed but performs full-resolution export; therefore quality acceptance must run against the final exported raster, not a reduced preview.
- Edge-aware sharpening after enlargement should be bounded and separately provenance-tracked. It can improve apparent detail while still creating halos or minimum-feature changes; rerun edge-displacement, halo, glyph/topology and printability QA after sharpening.

Protected storefront unchanged. No deployment, merge, or Oracle execution.
