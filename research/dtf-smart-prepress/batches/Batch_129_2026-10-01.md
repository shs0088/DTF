## Engineering synthesis delta
1. **Background removal must be decomposed into explicit artifacts.** Recommended chain: `CoarseMaskArtifact -> TrimapArtifact -> MatteArtifact(alpha) -> ForegroundRGB/DecontaminationArtifact -> Composite/ExportArtifact`. Each stage gets its own hash, parameters and QA status.
2. **Trimap geometry is model input, not disposable preprocessing.** Record unknown-band construction and invalidate downstream alpha results when the trimap-generation policy changes.
3. **Segmentation confidence needs a color-ambiguity escape path.** SIOX’s camouflage failure mode generalizes: low foreground/background separability should route to matting, alternate segmentation, or review instead of stronger morphology.
4. **Morphology must not become a semantic repair tool.** Erode/dilate/blur can clean masks but can also destroy thin DTF features; component count, holes, minimum stroke and boundary displacement must be compared before/after cleanup.
5. **Color enhancement is not color management.** Decorrelation/contrast transforms may improve visual separation but cannot substitute for source-profile assignment, ICC conversion, rendering intent, soft proof or gamut QA.
6. **Precision can be stage-specific.** Lower precision for coarse segmentation and higher precision for alpha estimation is a valid performance pattern, but precision/model/tiling choices must remain in provenance so preview and production paths cannot be conflated.
