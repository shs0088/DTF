## Engineering synthesis delta
- Final physical size is the authority for effective PPI and feature survival. Upscaling a small raster does not restore original edge evidence; a soft alpha edge also drives a soft/expanded RIP white boundary.
- Alpha quality and foreground-RGB quality remain separate. DTF edge cleanup needs alpha diagnostics plus bounded edge-color decontamination and multi-background compositing QA.
- White-underbase generation is an output/RIP concern by default. Explicit supplied white is an alternate authority; never silently stack supplied white/choke with RIP-generated white/choke.
- Halftone policy must be stored as a physical output artifact: method, LPI/cell geometry, dot/gap floor, tone mapping, and output condition. Error-diffusion/stochastic choices are not interchangeable with conventional screens.
- Edge cleanup must expose depth/band, outer-only vs internal-hole behavior, and border handling. These controls can change topology and therefore require minimum-feature/hole/island survival QA.
- Alpha-channel inspection should quantify opaque, semi-transparent and transparent populations and edge-zone statistics before any binary thresholding.
- Multiple matting/removal models should be routeable by subject/edge class; manual correction remains a separate override artifact rather than being folded into the automatic matte.
