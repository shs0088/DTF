1590. https://imagefader.com/en/ — Browser-local transparency/alpha editing documentation, opened/read directly. It distinguishes true alpha from a white visual overlay, preserves original export dimensions, exposes overall opacity, linear/radial/sector gradient fades, edge feathering and local mask edits, and recommends inspection over light/dark backgrounds. Engineering transfer: Smart Prepress should keep `AlphaEditArtifact` separate from RGB compositing, preserve original geometry, record mask/gradient parameters, and perform multi-background composite QA because a checkerboard or white preview alone can hide fringe/flattening defects.

1591. https://pandatoolbox.com/image-halftone-effect-generator/ — Halftone implementation page, opened/read directly. It divides the image into square cells, computes each cell's average brightness, and maps that tone to circle radius; grid size and maximum dot coverage are independent controls. Engineering transfer: cell-averaged dot screening is a distinct screening family from error diffusion and RIP AM/FM. Provenance must record cell/grid pitch, tone-to-radius mapping, maximum coverage and output geometry, and printability QA must compare resulting minimum dots/gaps against physical DTF limits.

1592. https://imageonline.io/halftone-maker/ — Halftone/screen implementation documentation, opened/read directly. It exposes single-ink, colour and CMYK screens, circle/square/line dot shapes, rotated lattices, screen angle, dot size and ink scaling; it also documents CMYK trade angles and explicit dot-gain compensation by shrinking requested dots. Engineering transfer: separate `ScreenGeometry` from `ToneCompensation`; store screen family, lattice angle, shape, physical pitch, coverage/tone curve and dot-gain compensation independently. A browser-scaled or 1000-pixel-normalized setting must be converted to final physical units before DTF acceptance, and any later resize invalidates the screen calibration.

## Engineering synthesis
1. Add an explicit `AlphaEditArtifact` stage for user-authored opacity/feather/gradient/local-mask edits. These edits are neither segmentation nor color compositing and must remain replayable with parameters and edit history.
2. Keep true alpha distinct from white/colored overlays. Export validation should reject accidental flattening when transparency is required and inspect the same RGBA over multiple backgrounds.
3. Expand screening taxonomy with `CELL_AVERAGED_DOT_SCREEN` alongside error diffusion, AM, FM and hybrid. Record cell pitch, dot shape, angle, tone-to-area/radius mapping and maximum coverage.
4. Separate screen geometry from tone/dot-gain compensation. Changing dot size to compensate gain is not the same operation as changing screen pitch or output scale.
5. Normalize all screening controls to final physical geometry. Pixel-normalized UI settings are preview conveniences only; after final print width/DPI is known, regenerate the screen and run minimum-dot/minimum-gap, tone, moire/anisotropy and white-underbase coupling QA.
