## Findings
- Thresholding must record family, not only a scalar: lower/upper, double-bound, multilevel, global/local, Otsu, Bernsen, Niblack and gradient/entropy variants have different behavior.
- Adaptive threshold window size is a print-detail parameter. Small windows can amplify noise; large windows approach global behavior. Record block geometry, weighting family, sigma and C.
- Morphology after thresholding must remain a separate artifact so component, hole, stroke and skeleton topology changes can be measured.
- Denoise routing should be noise-aware: Gaussian, median and bilateral filtering serve different artifact classes; unsharp masking is sharpening, not denoising.
- Resampling provenance must record method, direction and geometry. Nearest, linear, area, cubic and Lanczos4 use materially different neighborhoods. Resizing after halftone or white-mask generation invalidates those calibrated artifacts.
