## New materially distinct pages read

1564. https://github.com/MarkoUnity/NanoAlpha — Open-source browser/API alpha-recovery implementation. Read directly. Single-image route estimates a near-solid background in OKLab from border samples, flood-fills background-like regions, estimates fractional alpha in an edge band by inverse compositing, then decontaminates edge RGB and propagates foreground color. A second route accepts the same render against a contrasting background and recovers alpha from the observed/background differences. Engineering transfer: add a MULTI_PLATE_ALPHA_RECOVERY route for assets where matched renders exist; validate exact geometry alignment; separate alpha estimation from foreground-RGB decontamination; expose Alpha/Edge previews. Secondary security/async lesson: decode/process uploads in memory, enforce file-byte + decoded-megapixel + concurrency + rate limits, and record API/job limits in provenance.

1565. https://oneimagetool.com/fr/dither-effect — French Floyd–Steinberg 1-bit dithering explainer/tool. Read directly. Explains that quantization error is distributed to undecided neighbours so local average luminance survives, distinguishes error-diffusion dithering from fixed-grid halftoning, and notes that output scaling/display averaging can visually erase pixel-level texture. Engineering transfer: distinguish `ERROR_DIFFUSION` from `AM_HALFTONE`; preview at final physical scale and at 1:1 pixels; do not infer printability from a browser-resized preview.

1566. https://transferkingz.com/blogs/transfer-kingz-blog/white-ink-underbase-dtf — DTF white-underbase troubleshooting guide. Read directly. Separates object-area, image-area, no-fill and partial-transparency white generation; maps gray/patchy/streaked white to distinct causes including insufficient laydown, registration/choke, settled TiO2/clogs and bad artwork alpha; recommends controlled one-variable-at-a-time test prints. Engineering transfer: add `WhiteGenerationMode` and symptom-driven diagnosis that separates artwork/RIP geometry from mechanical ink-delivery failures; store named RIP presets and calibration test evidence rather than universal pixel/density defaults.

1567. https://dtfgears.com/blogs/news/digital-factory-v12-for-white-ink-underbase-printing-best-practice-settings/ — CADlink Digital Factory V12 white-underbase/screening guide. Read directly. Explicitly separates white density, choke/spread and screening, rejects a universal white percentage, and distinguishes AM screening (structured pattern/variable dot size), FM screening (smaller dots/varying frequency) and hybrid approaches. Recommends controlled calibration sheets with solids, photos, small text, thin lines, gradients and fine transparent details, holding artwork/film/cure/fabric constant while changing one RIP variable. Engineering transfer: make white density, geometry and screening independent profile dimensions; add AM/FM/HYBRID screening family provenance; calibration results must be tied to printer/ink/film/cure/fabric profile.

## Engineering synthesis
1. Add `BackgroundRemovalRoute = SEGMENTATION | TRIMAP_MATTING | KNOWN_BACKGROUND_UNMULT | MULTI_PLATE_ALPHA_RECOVERY`. Multi-plate recovery requires matched geometry and known/estimated background colors; mismatched renders must be rejected rather than silently combined.
2. Keep `AlphaGeometry` and `ForegroundRGBDecontamination` as separate stages. Edge RGB can remain contaminated even when alpha is numerically plausible; preview Result, Alpha and Edge bands independently and composite against multiple backgrounds.
3. Add `WhiteGenerationMode = NONE | IMAGE_AREA | OBJECT_AREA | PARTIAL_TRANSPARENCY/ALPHA_AWARE | EXPLICIT_SPOT`. Do not conflate where white is generated with white density, choke/spread or screening.
4. Expand `WhiteFailureDiagnosis` so artwork-alpha/RIP failures are distinguished from mechanical failures such as nozzle loss, pigment settling and circulation. Software must not recommend more choke for a streaking/nozzle problem.
5. Add `ScreeningFamily = ERROR_DIFFUSION | AM | FM | HYBRID` plus algorithm/kernel, dot/frequency parameters, tone compensation, final physical geometry and preview scale. Browser-scale appearance is not a printability measurement.
6. Calibration must be production-profile scoped: printer, resolution/pass strategy, ink set, film, powder/cure, garment/fabric and RIP version. Density/choke/screening numbers from third parties are evidence for test ranges, not universal constants.
7. Build controlled calibration sheets that contain large solids, small typography, thin strokes, gradients, photographic detail and fractional-alpha edges. Change one variable at a time and retain dated named presets plus rollback evidence.
8. For upload/API processing, enforce byte limits before decode, decoded-pixel limits after decode, concurrency/rate limits, in-memory processing where feasible, and immutable job-setting snapshots. This prevents decompression/resource abuse and makes asynchronous results reproducible.
