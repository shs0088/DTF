## New materially distinct pages read

1559. https://comfy.icu/node/RuiUnmult — Chinese/English ComfyUI Unmult Matting implementation documentation. Read directly. Solves known-solid-background composites algebraically to recover fractional alpha and foreground color, exposes black/white alpha remap points, and documents a critical failure mode: dark solid subject regions against black can be misinterpreted as low opacity. Engineering transfer: add a KnownBackgroundUnmult route only when background assumptions are validated; combine with a subject-protection mask for opaque dark regions; preserve fractional alpha for glow/smoke/particles; record the known background color and alpha remap settings.

1560. https://www.plotcaster.com/learn/floyd-steinberg-dithering/ — English Floyd–Steinberg implementation/analysis. Read directly. Documents the 7/16,3/16,5/16,1/16 kernel, floating-point error accumulation, serpentine scanning, worms/checkerboard/startup artifacts, high-pass sharpening behavior, linear-light/gamma considerations, dot-gain compensation and sequential-dependency limits for GPU parallelization. Engineering transfer: screening provenance must include scan order, working color/transfer domain and tone/dot-gain compensation; QA must inspect directional texture and edge overshoot rather than tone coverage alone.

1561. https://www.plotcaster.com/learn/atkinson-dithering/ — English Atkinson dithering implementation/analysis. Read directly. Distributes six 1/8 shares and deliberately discards one quarter of quantization error, increasing local contrast while losing highlight/shadow tone accuracy. Engineering transfer: Atkinson is an intentional stylization/contrast choice, not a neutral printability transform; preserve its error-retention policy in provenance and reject it as an automatic substitute for tone-faithful screening.

1562. https://habr.com/ru/articles/278435/ — Russian Bradley/Bradley-Roth adaptive image binarization tutorial. Read directly. Describes local adaptive thresholding rather than one global threshold and motivates it for images whose local illumination/background varies. Engineering transfer: threshold selection must be routed by spatial nonuniformity; local thresholding can be useful for contamination/speck diagnostics and mask proposals, but any binary conversion of artwork alpha remains destructive unless explicitly authorized and topology/minimum-feature QA passes.

1563. https://habr.com/ru/articles/863236/ — Russian C#/CodeLab implementation tutorial for Jarvis–Judice–Ninke 1-bit dithering. Read directly. Implements JJN error diffusion and discusses its multi-neighbour, sequential processing behavior and gamma/contrast/brightness controls. Engineering transfer: JJN belongs to the error-diffusion family but has a wider support than Floyd–Steinberg; store kernel/family and pre-tone controls, and treat thread/parallel execution changes as potentially output-changing because diffusion depends on processing order.

## Engineering synthesis
1. Add BackgroundRemovalRoute = SEGMENTATION | TRIMAP_MATTING | KNOWN_BACKGROUND_UNMULT. Unmult is exact only under a valid known-background/compositing assumption; dark-on-black opaque subjects require subject protection or a different route.
2. Add MattingProvenance for known-background recovery: background RGB, alpha black/white remap points, subject-protection mask identity and alpha convention. Preserve fractional alpha for emissive/smoke/particle assets.
3. Expand ScreeningProvenance with error-diffusion kernel, error-retention fraction, scan order/serpentine flag, working transfer domain (linear/perceptual), pre-tone controls and dot-gain compensation. These parameters materially change physical tone and texture.
4. Add ScreeningArtifactQA: directional anisotropy/worms, periodic/checkerboard texture, row-edge startup transients, edge overshoot/sharpening and highlight/shadow tone loss. Average coverage alone is insufficient.
5. Distinguish tone-faithful screening from stylization. Floyd–Steinberg attempts full error conservation; Atkinson intentionally discards error and therefore must not be silently substituted in a production profile.
6. Add ThresholdRouting based on illumination/background nonuniformity. Adaptive/local thresholds may generate diagnostic or proposal masks, but conversion of continuous artwork alpha to binary requires explicit authorization plus topology, holes, glyphs and minimum-physical-feature survival checks.
7. Error-diffusion execution order is part of the algorithm. Parallelization, tiling or asynchronous chunking must not alter row dependencies without a declared approximation mode and regression comparison.
