## Engineering synthesis

### 1. Alpha-matte generation and final compositing must remain separate artifacts
Microsoft's retired Image Analysis background-removal documentation explicitly distinguished an edited transparent foreground from the alpha matte itself. It also documents thin-detail artifacts and the latency tradeoff between full-resolution removal and downsampled matte generation. Smart Prepress should preserve `MatteArtifact` independently from the composited/export image, and any upscaled matte shortcut must trigger full-resolution boundary QA before print-master acceptance.

### 2. Distance transforms are a stronger geometric basis for choke/spread than repeated morphology
ImageMagick documents distance morphology, multiple distance metrics, anti-aliased-shape handling and the precision/range implications of integer versus floating-point storage. For DTF, white-underbase choke/spread should be generated from a high-precision distance field at final physical geometry, with the distance metric and numeric precision recorded. This avoids opaque repeated-erosion semantics and supports exact millimetre offsets plus minimum-feature guards.

### 3. Morphology iteration is not universally equivalent to a larger kernel
ImageMagick shows that repeated small convex kernels can approximate larger morphology efficiently, but warns that this equivalence fails for non-convex kernels and that convergence-style iteration can become runaway/expensive. Smart Prepress must record kernel identity and iteration semantics, cap convergence work, and never translate a requested physical choke blindly into an arbitrary iteration count.

### 4. Image QA needs metric families, not one universal score
`skimage.metrics` exposes MSE/NRMSE/PSNR/SSIM alongside segmentation-oriented measures such as Hausdorff distance, adapted Rand error and contingency-based metrics. DTF QA should route metrics by artifact: PSNR/SSIM for reconstruction diagnostics, Hausdorff/boundary displacement for masks and white geometry, and topology/component metrics for segmentation. A high global similarity score must not override a failed edge/minimum-feature test.

### 5. Deblurring is model-based restoration, not sharpening
The scikit-image Richardson–Lucy example requires a point-spread function and an iteration count. This reinforces the existing separation between `DEBLUR_RESTORATION` and `SHARPEN`: deconvolution must preserve PSF/model/iteration provenance and be checked for ringing, noise amplification, edge displacement, glyph topology and minimum strokes before acceptance.

### 6. Bilateral denoising has two independent scales
The Japanese bilateral-filter source derives separate Gaussian weights for spatial distance (`sigma_s`) and intensity/range distance (`sigma_r`). Treating these as one generic 'denoise strength' loses reproducibility and risks edge damage. `DenoiseArtifact` should store both scales, color/intensity space and kernel extent; alpha and RGB denoising authority should remain separate.
