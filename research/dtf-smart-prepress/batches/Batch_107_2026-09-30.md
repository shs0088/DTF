## New materially distinct pages read

1529. https://wutools.com/es/imagen/background-remover — Spanish. Background removal / alpha matting / edge contamination. Read directly. Key finding: semantic segmentation and soft alpha matting are separate stages; halo can remain in foreground RGB even when alpha geometry looks correct. Recommends edge decontamination/refinement for anti-aliased, hair, motion-blurred and translucent boundaries.

1530. https://snapcorn.com/resources/alpha-channels-and-matting — English. Alpha conventions and matting. Read directly. Key finding: straight-vs-premultiplied alpha mismatch can create dark fringes independent of segmentation quality; continuous matte differs materially from a binary mask.

1531. https://helpx.adobe.com/jp/after-effects/desktop/apply-effects-and-animation-presets/list-of-effects/noise-grain-effects.html — Japanese, Adobe official documentation. Read directly. Key finding: noise/grain operations may target RGB and alpha independently and support multiple bit depths; prepress processing must explicitly state channel scope and must not accidentally perturb print alpha while denoising RGB.

1532. https://www.bigimg.ai/ja — Japanese. AI enlargement/background-removal/denoise workflow. Read directly. Key finding: AI upscaling reconstructs inferred detail rather than merely resampling pixels; generated detail must therefore be treated as synthetic and subjected to glyph/topology/edge QA before print acceptance.

## Engineering synthesis
1. Add AlphaConvention = straight | premultiplied | unknown and a convention-normalization test before halo diagnosis.
2. Keep AlphaGeometry and ForegroundRGBDecontamination as separate QA dimensions; a clean matte can still carry old-background color in boundary RGB.
3. Every denoise/sharpen/filter operation must declare ChannelScope = RGB | ALPHA | BOTH. Default prepress cleanup should preserve alpha unless the operation is explicitly an alpha-refinement stage.
4. Treat AI super-resolution as synthesis, not neutral resize. Require post-SR glyph topology, small-feature, edge-position, color and alpha checks at final physical print size.
5. Continue multi-background compositing QA (black/white/neutral/garment-like) because convention mismatches and RGB contamination can remain invisible on a checkerboard.
