## Synthesis delta

- Add **Foreground RGB Edge Contamination QA** alongside alpha-edge QA. A visually correct alpha can still carry old-background RGB in fractional pixels.
- Preserve **SegmentationMask**, **Trimap**, **AlphaMatte**, and **RecoveredForegroundRGB** as separate artifacts with provenance.
- Add a routing decision based on boundary class: hard/opaque vs mixed/fractional/translucent.
- Extend final production QA beyond geometry: very small white-only features may survive choke yet still be mechanically weak because of limited adhesive/contact area.
- Prefer region-aware denoise/cleanup masks so background repair does not destroy subject contours, shadows, fine text, or minimum printable features.

No deployment, merge, storefront modification, or production-code change.
