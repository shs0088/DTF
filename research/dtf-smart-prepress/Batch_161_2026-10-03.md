## Newly verified materially distinct pages

1. NanoAlpha — open-source browser/API alpha extraction
   https://github.com/MarkoUnity/NanoAlpha
   - Single-image path: border sampling/clustering in OKLab, perceptual distance, border-connected flood fill, inverse-compositing alpha estimation, edge-RGB decontamination and contour color propagation.
   - Two-image path: same render against two different known backgrounds; observed background difference is used to recover alpha and foreground RGB.
   - Engineering implication: add a deterministic dual-background matting route when paired renders exist. It can provide stronger evidence than single-image segmentation for synthetic/mockup assets.
   - Security implication: enforce decoded-megapixel, byte-size, concurrency and rate limits independently; decode in memory and avoid persistence by default.

2. SR DTF — Contraer Bordes DTF Online
   https://srdtf.com/herramientas/contraer-bordes-dtf/
   - Calculates choke from final physical size/effective DPI rather than trusting PNG DPI metadata.
   - Separates low-alpha ghost cleanup, residual-background RGB decontamination, morphological erosion, controlled anti-aliasing and white-mask export.
   - Uses border flood-fill for white-background removal so legitimate internal white objects can survive.
   - Engineering implication: background cleanup, RGB decontamination and white-underbase choke are separate stages; minimum-feature survival must be evaluated after physical choke.

3. NestSheet — White Underbase with Choke
   https://nestsheet.com/white-underbase
   - Exposes alpha-derived underbase, choke, alpha threshold, white amount, standard/soft-hand behavior and halftone as separate controls with channel preview.
   - Engineering implication: underbase generation should be an inspectable artifact with its own preview/coverage metrics; alpha threshold, white amount, choke and screening must not collapse into one white-strength parameter.

4. Nouplo — Halftone Generator
   https://img.nouplo.com/halftone-generator/
   - Distinguishes dot/line screen geometry and screen coarseness; emphasizes transparent-output halftoning for DTF and local browser processing.
   - Engineering implication: store halftone family/shape/geometry separately from transparency and output resolution, and evaluate the resulting physical dot/gap sizes after final scaling.

## Synthesis delta

- Add DualBackgroundMattingArtifact: source plate, contrast plate, known/estimated background colors, registration check, recovered alpha, recovered foreground RGB, confidence/residual map.
- Prefer deterministic border-connected background removal for genuinely uniform backgrounds before invoking expensive semantic AI.
- Treat alpha cleanup, foreground RGB decontamination, white-mask generation, physical choke, and halftone/screening as distinct auditable stages.
- Add upload/runtime guards on both compressed input and decoded workload: bytes, width/height, megapixels, estimated memory, concurrency, timeout and rate limit.
- White-channel QA should expose the generated mask itself plus coverage and post-choke topology/minimum-feature survival, not only the final composite.
- Halftone QA must occur at final physical geometry; resizing a screened artifact changes dot and gap dimensions and therefore printability.

No deployment, merge, storefront modification, or production-code change.
