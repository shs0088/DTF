## Engineering synthesis delta

### 1. White generation is a transfer function, not merely a binary alpha mask
Caldera documents white strategies where underlay amount can depend on opacity/transparency and color, plus minimum/maximum white, white-highlight thresholds, and opacity-reduction behavior. Smart Prepress therefore must model the downstream RIP's white-generation policy explicitly rather than assuming every visible source pixel receives identical white.

Recommended fields:
- white_generation_strategy
- alpha_to_white_transfer
- min_white_pct / max_white_pct
- white_color_threshold
- low_alpha_suppression
- rip_profile_id

### 2. Choke and spread are not interchangeable
Choke subtracts from the white plate and remains inside the source bounds. Spread expands the plate and can be clipped by the source/image bounding box. Preflight should therefore detect insufficient transparent padding before a workflow that expects outward spread.

Recommended checks:
- transparent_margin_mm per edge
- requested_spread_mm
- spread_clipping_risk
- post_choke feature survival

### 3. Low-alpha contamination needs two separate repairs
The source evidence reinforces the distinction between (a) unwanted alpha coverage and (b) RGB contamination retained in semi-transparent boundary pixels. Alpha cleanup and RGB edge decontamination must remain separate operations and be limited to an analyzed boundary band.

### 4. Underbase QA must simulate the target RIP policy
A generic white preview is insufficient when RIP white generation uses thresholds or opacity-dependent white. Generate both:
- neutral alpha-derived white preview; and
- RIP-profile-derived white preview.

Flag their delta so operators can see where a seemingly harmless alpha/color change creates or removes actual white-ink events.

### 5. Preserve physical units
Vendor pixel-based choke examples are not portable across output resolution or print size. Store choke/spread/minimum feature in mm and derive pixels only after final physical placement and output raster resolution are known.

## Pipeline update
decode/validate -> normalize metadata/color -> establish final physical placement -> segmentation/matting -> topology analysis -> boundary RGB decontamination when justified -> alpha cleanup when justified -> RIP-aware white generation -> physical choke/spread simulation -> post-operation feature survival -> final-resolution sharpening -> multi-background QA + neutral white preview + RIP-profile white preview -> ICC/RIP handoff.
