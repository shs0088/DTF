## Accepted materially distinct pages

1. https://github.com/gmaynez/lanczos-scale-plugin/
   - Topic: alpha-safe resampling, Lanczos/Kaiser/EWA, linear-light filtering.
   - Engineering finding: RGBA resampling should premultiply alpha before filtering and unpremultiply afterward. Filter choice is content-dependent; Lanczos3 can ring at high-contrast edges, while wider/alternative kernels trade crispness for alias rejection.

2. https://www.mybay.it/content/26-la-tecnica-dtf-guida-tecnica-completa-alla-stampa-direct-to-film
   - Language: Italian.
   - Topic: DTF white underbase/choke and fine-detail risks.
   - Engineering finding: white choke belongs to RIP/output calibration rather than being painted into artwork. Excessive choke can consume fine text/detail and weaken edge coverage. Vendor numeric suggestions are calibration starting points, not universal DTF standards.

3. https://helpx.adobe.com/uk/photoshop/desktop/make-selections/refine-modify-selections/fringe-pixels-around-a-selection.html
   - Topic: fringe/halo formation at anti-aliased boundaries.
   - Engineering finding: partially covered anti-aliased edge pixels can retain the old background RGB and become visible after compositing on a different background. Alpha quality and RGB edge contamination therefore require separate QA.

4. https://whitebackgroundremover.com/ar/
   - Language: Arabic.
   - Topic: white-background removal, tolerance, edge smoothing, denoise, multi-background preview.
   - Engineering finding: deterministic/color-oriented background removal exposes useful independent controls (tolerance, smoothing, denoise, edge detection). Multi-background preview is useful QA for revealing halos that checkerboard-only preview can hide.

## Synthesis update
- Resampling stage should be alpha-aware: straight RGBA -> premultiply -> filter in an appropriate working space -> unpremultiply -> edge QA.
- Record `resampler_family`, `kernel/support`, `working_light_space`, `alpha_filtering_mode`, and scale ratio in provenance.
- Add ringing/halo QA around high-contrast edges after upscale/downscale.
- Keep `alpha_transition_quality` separate from `edge_rgb_contamination`.
- White choke remains an output-profile parameter linked to printer/RIP/ink/film/mode calibration, not a universal artwork constant.
- Background-removal QA should composite against at least transparent/checkerboard, white, black, and a saturated color to expose fringe contamination.
