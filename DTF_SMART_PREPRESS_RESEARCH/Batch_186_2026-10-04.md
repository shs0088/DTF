## Engineering synthesis

### 1. Choke must be expressed in physical units first
SR DTF explicitly computes choke against final physical print size/effective DPI rather than trusting embedded PNG DPI. Proposed representation:
- choke_mm
- effective_print_dpi
- derived_choke_px
- final_physical_width_mm / height_mm

Pixel-only choke is not portable across resizing/output conditions.

### 2. Separate ghost-alpha cleanup from legitimate fractional transparency
Low-alpha fringe, residual antialias and background color matting can create printable filaments/halos, but legitimate shadows/gradients must survive. Edge cleanup therefore needs classification before thresholding. Add:
- alpha_edge_class = ghost | antialias | legitimate_transparency | uncertain
- edge_rgb_contamination_score
- alpha_threshold_applied
- decontamination_method
- fractional_alpha_preservation_score

### 3. Choke alone is insufficient for semi-transparent gradients
The Turkish source demonstrates that an opaque-artwork choke can solve edge white protrusion while still failing on transparency-to-opacity gradients if the RIP generates white under every nonzero-alpha pixel. White generation policy and white density are separate controls. Add:
- white_generation_mode = binary/nonzero-alpha | alpha-weighted | color/gradient-aware | explicit-spot
- white_density
- semi_transparent_white_policy

### 4. Defect routing must distinguish digital prepress from machine/process faults
White problems can originate in RIP/channel generation, but film/powder/ink compatibility, storage, white circulation, nozzle state and registration can produce similar symptoms. Portuguese smear guidance further separates artwork blur from carriage-height, wet-ink/white-density, speed and head-alignment faults. Smart Prepress should report “file-side evidence absent; inspect process” rather than destructively sharpening/eroding artwork.

Suggested fields:
- defect_origin_probability: artwork / RIP / registration / ink-load / mechanical / consumable / cure-transfer
- nozzle_check_state
- head_alignment_state
- carriage_clearance_state
- white_ink_load
- media_consumable_profile
