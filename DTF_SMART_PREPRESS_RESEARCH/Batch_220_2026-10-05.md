## Engineering synthesis
- Dedithering is a cleanup pass distinct from quantization. Neighbor-support cleanup can remove isolated printable islands but can also erase intentional stipple and fine detail; QA must compare topology and feature survival before/after each pass.
- White-underbase choke must be specified in physical units against effective print DPI, not blindly against PNG metadata DPI. Edge cleanup, RGB decontamination, alpha thresholding, morphology, and white-mask generation remain separate stages.
- Border-connected background removal differs from global white/color knockout and preserves legitimate internal whites more safely.
- Halftone controls such as dot size, angle, and density are independent output-condition variables. Visual preview is not sufficient; minimum printable dot/island survival must be calibrated to printer/RIP/film/ink conditions.
- Matting estimates continuous alpha and ideally foreground color; segmentation alone cannot solve foreground RGB contamination. Multi-background compositing QA remains mandatory for mockup-safe assets.
- Compressed or low-resolution boundary pixels reduce matting reliability; model-input dimensions and any pre-inference resize must be recorded.
