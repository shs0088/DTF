## Engineering synthesis
- Alpha-bounds trimming must be a non-resampling canvas operation. Any alpha > 0 may extend bounds, so faint stray alpha can cause unexpectedly large artwork geometry; preflight should report strict bounds and thresholded/practical bounds separately.
- Browser-local matting is viable for privacy, but model-domain routing matters. Portrait-specialized MODNet should not be treated as a general product/logo model. Model working resolution and alpha upsampling can lose very fine detail even when output pixel dimensions match the source.
- Real transparency/feathering must be distinguished from blending to white. QA should composite the same RGBA asset over light, dark, and garment-representative backgrounds.
- Anti-aliased boundary pixels can retain original-background RGB and create fringe/halo after recomposition. Alpha geometry cleanup and foreground-RGB decontamination remain separate stages.
