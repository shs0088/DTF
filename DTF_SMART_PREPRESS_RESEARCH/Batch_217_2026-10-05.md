## Synthesis updates
- Treat background removal as at least two distinct deterministic/AI families: border-connected removal versus global color-key removal. Their failure modes differ, especially for legitimate internal colors.
- Add topology-aware QA after background removal: enclosed holes, islands, counters in letters, and connected-component preservation.
- Add `edge_failure_class = alpha_geometry | foreground_rgb_spill | compression_artifact | low_effective_resolution | model/domain_failure | unknown`.
- Despill must be edge/alpha gated and color-aware; it must not globally suppress legitimate garment/artwork colors.
- Screening provenance: `output_dpi`, `physical_size`, `screen_lpi`, `screen_angle`, `minimum_dot_mm`, `dot_edge_antialias`, separation mode, gamma/cut/tolerance/transition controls.
- A minimum-dot warning from any vendor is not a universal DTF threshold. Calibrate per printer/RIP/ink/film/powder/press/output mode.
- Targeted halftone requires per-color/swatch masks and preservation QA for colors outside the selected masks.
- Any resize after halftoning/screening invalidates screening QA and requires regeneration at final physical geometry.
