## Synthesis update
### Alpha-matte quality must be multi-metric
For DTF edge QA, record at minimum:
- alpha_sad
- alpha_mse
- alpha_gradient_error
- alpha_connectivity_error
- edge-band error
- topology/component/hole deltas
- multi-background fringe score

MSE alone can hide structurally bad boundaries; gradient and connectivity metrics expose edge and topology failures that matter directly to DTF cutouts.

### Matting router refinement
Recommended routing remains:
existing valid alpha -> audit;
simple separable background -> deterministic removal;
opaque complex subject -> segmentation;
portrait/hair/fur/translucency -> alpha matting;
trimap available -> classical or neural trimap-conditioned matting;
uncertain output -> human/refinement path.

### Local execution
Client-side ONNX/WASM/WebGPU matting can reduce privacy and upload risk. It does not remove resource-safety requirements: decoded-pixel limits, memory budgeting, model version pinning, timeout/cancellation, and output validation remain mandatory.
