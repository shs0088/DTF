## Newly verified pages

1. https://halftoneapp.com/
   - Interactive DTF separation/halftone workflow exposing target physical size, working/output DPI, luminance-vs-garment-color separation, gamma/cut/tolerance/transition controls, LPI, angle, minimum physical dot and anti-aliasing.
   - Engineering implication: printability decisions must bind raster geometry to physical scale; minimum-dot checks belong after target size and raster DPI are known.

2. https://dtfnestly.com/blog/w1-white-channel-rip/
   - Distinguishes RIP-generated white from an explicit per-pixel white spot plane; documents named W1 spot-channel workflow and TIFF/8BIM channel metadata.
   - Engineering implication: Smart Prepress needs a WhitePlaneSource enum (auto-from-alpha vs explicit spot plane), spot-channel naming/metadata validation, and a preflight check that the intended white separation survives export/import.

3. https://dtfdatabase.com/blog/dtf-prints-faded-dull-colors-fix/
   - Separates defects by timeline: weak/dull immediately after printing/pressing versus fading after washing. Immediate dullness can arise from weak/patchy white, RIP/ICC configuration, ink/nozzle health, or over-pressing; later fading points toward migration/cure/bond/wash durability.
   - Engineering implication: defect diagnosis should include observation stage/time, not only image appearance. This prevents artwork processing from masking hardware/process failures.

4. https://dtfgangsheetapp.com/auto-spot-channels
   - Describes adaptive choke based on edge complexity, minimum feature width and design size rather than one fixed contraction for a mixed gang sheet; exports white as named spot separations in multiple container formats.
   - Engineering implication: choke must be feature-aware and topology-safe. A useful hard guard is that inward contraction cannot consume the local feature; compute local width/medial-radius and constrain the operation before generating the white plane.

## Synthesis updates

### 1. White-plane semantics are first-class data
Add:
- white_plane_source = alpha_generated | explicit_spot | external_mask
- white_spot_name
- spot_metadata_valid
- white_plane_bit_depth
- white_plane_density_mapping
- export_container
- rip_mapping_verified

A transparent RGBA artwork and an explicit W1 separation are not semantically equivalent when semi-sheer regions or deliberate white omissions exist.

### 2. Choke becomes spatially constrained, not a global scalar
Compute local feature width / medial radius and edge complexity before contracting the white mask. Record both requested and actually applied contraction. Clamp or reject choke where it would destroy a stroke, counter, serif, hair-like detail or small text component.

Suggested fields:
- requested_choke_mm
- applied_choke_map
- local_feature_width_min_mm
- topology_guard_triggered
- components_before_after
- holes_before_after
- skeleton_endpoint_delta

### 3. Physical-dot survivability is downstream of final geometry
Halftone/separation QA should bind:
target physical size + raster DPI + LPI + angle + minimum dot size + alpha edge treatment.
Any later resize invalidates the previous survivability result and forces re-screen/re-QA.

### 4. Defect diagnosis gains a time/stage axis
Add observation_stage:
source_preview | RIP_preview | film_prepress | transfer_postpress | after_wash.
This sharply narrows causal routing and prevents inappropriate digital correction of nozzle, cure, migration or bonding problems.
