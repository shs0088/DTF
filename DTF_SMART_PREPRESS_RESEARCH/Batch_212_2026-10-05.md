## Engineering synthesis update

### White underbase
Model white generation as at least four independent dimensions:
- support/eligibility function (which pixels receive white),
- density function (how much white),
- geometry operation (exact/choke/spread),
- optional screening/halftone.

A single "white amount" or "choke" slider cannot represent semi-transparent fades correctly.

### Physical geometry
Store choke/spread in physical units first. Derive pixel radius only after final physical dimensions and output DPI are known. Embedded PNG DPI is advisory metadata and must not override explicit final print dimensions.

### Edge cleanup
Keep these QA stages separate:
1. alpha geometry/threshold cleanup,
2. morphological contraction/expansion,
3. foreground RGB edge decontamination,
4. anti-alias/fractional-alpha reconstruction,
5. underbase generation,
6. minimum-stroke/topology survival checks.

### Quality metrics
PSNR/SSIM can be auxiliary regression metrics, but DTF acceptance additionally needs alpha-boundary displacement, halo/ringing, thin-feature survival, topology preservation, text readability, color error, white-support coverage and simulated garment composite checks.

### RIP provenance
Persist output-condition identity with every final preflight result: RIP/profile identifier, printer/print mode, output DPI, white policy, choke/spread calibration and screening parameters. Changing any of these can invalidate downstream QA.
