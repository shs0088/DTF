## Engineering synthesis additions

### 1. Morphology must be scale- and topology-aware
A structuring element is not merely an implementation detail. Erosion can destroy thin bridges; dilation can merge nearby components; opening/closing can therefore alter topology even when their visual effect appears minor. Record kernel shape/size in physical units and gate morphology with component, hole, skeleton, endpoint, and minimum-stroke survival checks.

### 2. Separate deterministic background removal from semantic matting
For simple flat or near-flat backgrounds, target-color+tolerance removal is deterministic, fast, and auditable. Complex edges, low contrast, hair/fur, transparency, or foreground/background color overlap should route to segmentation/matting. Always expose the alpha mask and preview against multiple contrasting backgrounds.

### 3. Bilateral denoising has two independent scales
Bilateral filtering weights neighbors by both spatial distance (sigma_s) and intensity/range difference (sigma_r). These parameters control different failure modes and must not be collapsed into one generic “strength” slider. DTF QA should measure edge-gradient retention, thin-stroke survival, texture retention, and alpha-boundary change after denoising.

### 4. AI segmentation resolution creates a hidden edge-resolution ceiling
The Yuustudio implementation describes normalization to 1024×1024, production of a one-channel probability mask, and resizing that mask back to original dimensions. Therefore “output at original resolution” does not imply that alpha geometry was inferred at original resolution. Record model input resolution separately from export resolution and flag large scale-up ratios for edge refinement/review.

### 5. Browser-side inference is viable but needs explicit resource routing
WebGPU with WASM fallback, Web Worker execution, model caching, and transferable ArrayBuffers provide a practical local-processing architecture. The prepress router should record backend, model version, model-input dimensions, inference time, fallback state, and memory budget. Large images should not be silently downsampled without provenance.
