## Engineering synthesis
- White generation must be modeled as a strategy, not merely alpha erosion. Caldera exposes strategy-dependent white density, min/max white, white-as-transparency, highlight thresholds, spread/choke, smart choke, and an opacity-reduction coefficient for stray low-alpha drops.
- Spread has a canvas/bounding-box dependency: it cannot expand beyond the source image bounds. Prepress must therefore ensure transparent padding before outward spread and record canvas geometry.
- Turkish DTF guidance demonstrates that ordinary choke can handle opaque boundaries yet fail on semi-transparent gradient edges. White-density amount and the policy deciding where white is generated are independent controls.
- Color management is output-condition specific. ICC/output calibration must be tied to printer + ink + film + mode/revision; linearization/TVI, channel limits, GCR/UCR and white behavior belong to the calibrated output profile rather than universal artwork rules.
- Halftone preprocessing should check effective resolution at final physical size before screening. Artifact cleanup, sharpening and upscaling are distinct preprocessing operations; upscaling cannot recreate missing source detail.

## Proposed implementation deltas
- Add white_generation_strategy, white_min/max, white_as_transparency_threshold, white_highlight_threshold, opacity_reduction_coefficient.
- Add spread_canvas_margin_px/mm and reject/warn when requested spread is clipped by image bounds.
- Add semi_transparent_edge_white_policy and compare white support against continuous alpha, not only binary geometry.
- Bind ICC/linearization/TVI/GCR/UCR/ink limits to output_calibration_profile_id.
- Keep effective-DPI QA ahead of halftone generation and record all preprocessing provenance.
