## Engineering synthesis
- Prefer topology/attribute-aware cleanup over blind fixed-radius morphology where possible. Area opening/closing removes structures based on component area rather than a fixed footprint; this can better preserve long thin legitimate strokes than indiscriminate erosion/opening.
- Treat connectivity (4- vs 8-neighborhood), footprint, border mode, area/diameter thresholds and physical pixel scale as provenance. These choices alter holes, islands and fine strokes.
- Threshold selection must follow image statistics/illumination assumptions. Histogram-based and local methods solve different problems; compare candidate masks using topology and boundary QA rather than selecting one universal threshold.
- Error-diffusion halftoning propagates quantization error and differs structurally from ordered/Bayer dithering. Floyd–Steinberg and Atkinson produce different spatial/error behavior, so the algorithm must be output-condition scoped.
- Dither/halftone QA must occur at final physical geometry. Resizing after screening changes dot size and may invalidate calibrated dot-survivability limits.
- Browser-local processing is useful for privacy, but implementation/runtime constraints must never silently force resolution reduction.

## Proposed Smart Prepress additions
- AttributeMorphologyQA: component_area, hole_area, diameter, connectivity, skeleton/min-stroke preservation.
- ThresholdExperiment record: method, preprocessing, neighborhood/window, selected threshold, topology deltas.
- Screening provenance: family, algorithm, scan order, threshold/palette, final raster DPI, final physical scale.
- Reject or warn when morphology/thresholding deletes protected counters, disconnects strokes, or changes topology beyond configured tolerance.
