## Synthesis delta
- Add a strict distinction between output canvas resolution and native matte inference resolution.
- Add subject-aware model routing and confidence downgrade for fine features when matte inference is low-resolution.
- Make manual semantic correction, alpha-edge smoothing, RGB decontamination and underbase morphology independently traceable operations.
- Choke/spread should be physically defined (mm) and converted at effective print scale, not driven by metadata DPI.
- Preserve existing policy: vendor thresholds/presets are calibration hints only; final printability limits must come from printer + RIP + ink + film/media + print-mode calibration.
