## Engineering synthesis delta
- Split artifact-alpha cleanup from underbase geometry. A choke operation cannot substitute for detecting disconnected/low-alpha contamination.
- Add a low-alpha component audit before white generation: alpha histogram, connected-component area, distance from intended foreground, edge continuity, and intentional-soft-effect protection.
- Keep white density, choke/spread, edge policy, and screening method as independent calibrated parameters.
- Preserve output semantics in provenance: generated-white vs named spot W1, hard/soft edge policy, ICC profile, TIFF compression, RIP profile, printer/ink/film/powder/garment condition.
- Add calibration provenance: test-chart ID, parameter range, increment, selected value, operator/date, and output-condition fingerprint.
- Do not promote vendor numeric examples to universal DTF standards; learn thresholds from controlled physical test prints.
