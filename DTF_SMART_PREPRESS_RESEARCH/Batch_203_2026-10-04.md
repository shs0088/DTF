## Engineering synthesis

### Halftone/dither integrity
Floyd–Steinberg is error diffusion rather than a hard threshold: quantization error is propagated to neighboring pixels. The source explicitly notes PNG preserves the resulting 1-bit pattern more cleanly than JPEG, whose compression can soften it. Treat screened/dithered output as a terminal raster artifact: lossy recompression or resizing after screening invalidates screening QA.

Add/retain:
- halftone_family
- error_diffusion_kernel
- scan_order
- halftone_output_bit_depth
- post_halftone_resize_detected
- lossy_post_halftone_encoding_detected
- screening_qa_valid

### White underbase is an independently inspectable channel
NestSheet derives white from artwork alpha but exposes alpha threshold, choke, amount, standard/soft-hand behavior, halftoning and direct white-channel preview. This reinforces that source alpha and generated white support are not the same artifact. Store underbase provenance independently and make the generated channel previewable before RIP/export.

Add/retain:
- white_source_mode
- white_alpha_threshold
- white_amount
- underbase_geometry_mode
- white_halftone_mode
- underbase_channel_preview
- underbase_coverage_fraction

### Choke must be physical/output-condition aware
Aero Print describes choke as a negative offset and warns that excess choke can erase thin strokes/serifs. The exact suggested value is provider/process-specific, not a universal DTF standard. Internally normalize choke to physical units and derive pixels only from the final output DPI. Re-run minimum-feature survival after choke.

Add/retain:
- choke_physical_mm
- choke_pixels_at_output_dpi
- minimum_feature_survival_after_choke
- small_text_survival_after_choke
- white_halo_risk
- output_condition_id

## Architecture consequence
The prepress graph should enforce:
source/master -> geometry/final physical size -> alpha QA -> white-support generation -> physical choke -> optional white halftone/screening -> channel QA -> export/RIP.
Any resize after choke/screening must invalidate downstream QA and regenerate those derived channels.
