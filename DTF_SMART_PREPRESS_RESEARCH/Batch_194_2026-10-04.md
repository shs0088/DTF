## New verified pages
1. Russian — Habr, “Простой фильтр для автоматического удаления фона с изображений”
   https://habr.com/en/articles/353890/
   - Pipeline: gradient/edge mask -> outside flood fill -> morphology -> binarization/blur -> alpha.
   - Important failure modes: Sobel response is resolution-dependent with fixed kernels; flood fill can leak through contour gaps; holes/internal background regions may remain; shadows can survive.
   - Engineering implication: normalize edge/morphology scale to final physical scale, test topology, and never trust outside flood fill without leak/hole detection.

2. OpenCV 4.12 Alpha Matting
   https://docs.opencv.org/4.12.0/d4/d40/group__alphamat.html
   - Information Flow alpha matting consumes RGB + grayscale trimap (foreground/background/unknown) and estimates continuous opacity in unknown regions.
   - Engineering implication: preserve uncertainty as a trimap/boundary band rather than prematurely thresholding segmentation to binary alpha.

3. French — Invistools Image Dithering
   https://invistools.com/fr/image-dithering/
   - Documents Floyd–Steinberg error diffusion weights and distinguishes it from Atkinson and ordered Bayer approaches.
   - Engineering implication: record dithering family/parameters explicitly; screening/dithering is not interchangeable, and resize after pattern generation invalidates output QA.

4. BD Remove Background / ComfyUI Cloud
   https://comfy.icu/node/BD_RemoveBackground
   - Separates semantic mask generation, negative-mask subtraction, optional closed-form matting, trimap erosion/dilation, hole filling, decontamination, guided/VitMatte edge refinement, halo shrink, and black/white composites.
   - Engineering implication: model background removal as staged mask -> uncertainty band -> matting -> decontamination -> multi-background QA, with each stage independently auditable.

### Key architectural rule
Do not collapse segmentation, matting, decontamination, and morphology into one “remove background” operation. Preserve intermediate masks and uncertainty. Binary thresholding should occur only when the target print/output representation truly requires it.
