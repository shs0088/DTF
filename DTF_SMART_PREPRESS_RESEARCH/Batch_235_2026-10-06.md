## Synthesis delta

- Add a white-support coverage QA metric: compare color-support geometry against the post-choke white mask and flag unsupported edge pixels/features.
- Keep white geometry, white density, choke/spread and screening as independent controls.
- Preserve continuous alpha for translucent material and fine structures; do not binarize merely because a transparent PNG is required.
- Record dithering/screening algorithm and parameters in provenance rather than reducing all screened outputs to a generic "halftone" label.
- Maintain the rule that vendor numeric settings are starting points only; printer/RIP/ink/film/mode calibration remains authoritative.
