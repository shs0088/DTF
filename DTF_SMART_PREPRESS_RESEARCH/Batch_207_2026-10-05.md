## Newly accepted pages

1. OpenCV 4.12 — Image Thresholding
   https://docs.opencv.org/4.12.0/d7/d4d/tutorial_py_thresholding.html
   - Separates global, adaptive mean/Gaussian, and Otsu thresholding.
   - Adaptive thresholding derives a local threshold from a neighborhood; useful where illumination/background varies.
   - Otsu optimizes a global threshold for bimodal histograms; denoising before Otsu can materially change the result.
   - Engineering: threshold provenance must include method, neighborhood/block size, C, prefilter, and measured topology/thin-feature deltas.

2. OpenCV 3.4.6 — Foreground Extraction using GrabCut
   https://docs.opencv.org/3.4.6/dd/dfc/tutorial_js_grabcut.html
   - GrabCut distinguishes sure/probable foreground/background, learns GMM foreground/background distributions, then graph-cuts iteratively.
   - Human strokes are hard labels and refinement can continue in later iterations.
   - Engineering: preserve uncertainty classes before final alpha/matting; store manual hard-label edits separately from inferred segmentation.

3. GIMP 2.10 — Sharpen (Unsharp Mask)
   https://docs.gimp.org/2.10/en/gimp-filter-unsharp-mask.html
   - Radius, amount and threshold are independent controls.
   - Documentation recommends sharpening at final resolution.
   - Excessive blur/subtraction can create complementary-color or dark halos.
   - Sharpening only Value in HSV is suggested to reduce color distortion.
   - Engineering: sharpening is output-resolution dependent and needs halo/ringing and chroma-shift QA.

4. OpenCV 4.11 — Smoothing Images
   https://docs.opencv.org/4.11.0/d4/d13/tutorial_py_filtering.html
   - Low-pass filtering removes high-frequency content but can also blur edges.
   - Median filtering is useful for impulse-like noise; bilateral filtering combines spatial and intensity-distance weighting to preserve edges better than ordinary Gaussian smoothing, at greater computational cost.
   - Engineering: denoise routing must be noise-type/content-aware, with edge/thin-feature preservation metrics.

## Synthesis updates

- Thresholding must not be a single slider. Record threshold_method={global,adaptive_mean,adaptive_gaussian,otsu}, prefilter, block_size, C, and threshold value where applicable.
- Denoising and thresholding interact: changing the prefilter can change the segmentation topology. QA must therefore run after the full preprocessing+threshold chain.
- Binary segmentation and alpha matting remain separate stages. GrabCut's probable classes are useful uncertainty evidence and should not be discarded prematurely.
- Manual segmentation corrections are provenance-bearing hard labels; after edits, rerun topology, thin-feature, fractional-alpha, and edge-contamination QA.
- Sharpen only after final physical geometry/resampling. Record radius/amount/threshold and detect halo/ringing, chroma shift, noise amplification, and minimum-stroke damage.
- Denoising should be routed by defect type. Bilateral is edge-aware but not automatically safe for fine DTF artwork; compare edge displacement and minimum-stroke survival before/after.
