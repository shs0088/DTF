## Newly verified pages

1. OpenCV — Alpha Matting
Canonical: https://docs.opencv.org/4.11.0/d4/d40/group__alphamat.html
Topic: information-flow alpha matting, trimaps, soft boundaries.
Finding: the input trimap explicitly separates foreground/background/unknown pixels; unknown pixels are mixtures and are solved using pixel affinities. For Smart Prepress, uncertainty must remain first-class instead of being prematurely binarized.

2. DTFWiz — DTF White Ink Checker
Canonical: https://dtfwiz.com/tools/white-ink-checker
Topic: physical print-size/choke survival of thin details and halftone dots.
Finding: choke can erase thin strokes/dots from both sides; printability therefore depends on physical feature width at the requested output size, not source pixels alone. Halftone dots must pass the same underbase-survival test as text and linework. Vendor numeric ranges are treated as process guidance, not universal standards.

3. NestSheet — White Underbase with Choke
Canonical: https://www.nestsheet.com/white-underbase
Topic: alpha-derived white support, threshold, choke, white amount, and white-channel dithering.
Finding: alpha threshold, edge choke, overall white amount, and screening are independent controls. Semi-transparent anti-aliased/glow/remove-bg remnants can silently earn white support and create dusty halos. The actual generated white channel should be previewed and measured, not inferred from the color artwork alone.

## Engineering synthesis update

### Uncertainty-preserving alpha pipeline
Keep trimap/uncertainty states through matting:
`foreground/background/unknown -> alpha estimation -> foreground RGB estimation/decontamination -> composite QA`.
Do not collapse unknown pixels to a binary mask before matting.

Recommended provenance additions:
- trimap_source
- trimap_unknown_fraction
- matting_algorithm
- alpha_fractional_fraction
- foreground_estimation_method
- edge_decontamination_method

### Physical feature-survival gate
Add a post-sizing printability test after final physical dimensions and output condition are known:
- minimum_stroke_mm
- minimum_island_diameter_mm
- white_choke_mm
- post_choke_surviving_area_ratio
- critical_text_survival
- halftone_dot_survival
- distressed_texture_survival

A feature can be visually present in CMYK but fail because its white-support island disappears after choke.

### White support is not one slider
Represent white generation as separate stages:
1. alpha/support qualification (including alpha threshold or support function)
2. density/amount
3. geometry (exact/choke/spread)
4. screening/dithering
5. output-channel encoding
6. preview/QA against garment background

Do not hard-code vendor-specific pixel or LPI values as universal DTF standards. Convert physical calibration values to output pixels only after target DPI/print mode are known.

### Dusty-halo detector
Low-alpha edge remnants need a dedicated test because they may be visually subtle in the transparent PNG yet still trigger white ink and adhesive. Evaluate:
- low-alpha connected components near the silhouette
- white-support granted to low-alpha pixels
- edge RGB contamination
- simulated white-channel composite on dark garment
- post-choke/post-threshold topology
