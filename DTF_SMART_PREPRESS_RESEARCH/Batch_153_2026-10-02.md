## Engineering synthesis
- Treat white-density, choke/spread, cure/press conditions, and registration as separate output-condition variables; do not encode a universal preset into the master.
- White quality can be measured objectively in CIELAB after cure/press; production QA should support before/after measurements rather than visual inspection alone.
- Choke must be represented physically and calibrated per output condition. Pixel values are meaningful only with their output raster geometry.
- RIP behavior is device/profile dependent. White generation from source opacity means fractional-alpha artwork can produce fractional white support; this must be previewed against target garment colors.
- Printer headline DPI is not artwork effective PPI. Preserve separate fields for artwork effective PPI, RIP/output resolution, pass count, white density, and screening.
- Choke and spread are opposite geometric operations and must not be collapsed into a generic edge control.
- Fine-detail survival must be rechecked after white generation/choke, not only on the source master.
