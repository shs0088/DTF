1. Adobe Experience Manager / Dynamic Media (Portuguese) — resMode
Canonical: https://experienceleague.adobe.com/pt-br/docs/dynamic-media-developer-resources/image-serving-api/image-rendering-api/http-protocol-reference/http-protocol-command-reference/r-ir-http-resmode
Finding: bilinear, bicubic, modified Lanczos (sharp2), and bicubic-sharper are explicitly distinct resampling choices; modified Lanczos can be sharper but costs more CPU, and sharp2 replaced sharp to reduce smoothing/moire artifacts.

2. Armert Labs (Turkish) — Morphology: erosion, dilation, opening, closing
Canonical: https://python.armert.com.tr/50-goruntu-isleme/morfoloji/
Finding: erosion can remove isolated specks but also breaks thin bridges; dilation fills small holes and can join nearby components; opening removes external speck noise while closing repairs small holes/gaps. Structuring-element geometry is part of the operation and must be recorded.

3. WuTools (French) — background-removal edge halos
Canonical: https://wutools.com/fr/image/suppresseur-arriere-plan
Finding: imperfect alpha at antialiased, motion-blurred, hair, and semi-transparent boundaries can retain the old background color. Alpha geometry and RGB edge-color decontamination must be assessed separately.

4. UniFOA / Tudo é Ciência (Portuguese) — quantitative traditional super-resolution comparison
Canonical: https://conferencias.unifoa.edu.br/tc/article/view/2508
Finding: compares bicubic, Lanczos, bicubic+sharpen, and bicubic+unsharp-mask reconstruction with PSNR/SSIM. Prepress QA should compare resampling and sharpening as separate pipeline choices rather than calling all enlargement "upscaling".

## Engineering synthesis delta
- Morphology must be topology-aware. Record operation, structuring-element shape/size, iteration count, and before/after connected-component, hole, minimum-stroke, and thin-bridge survival metrics.
- Never use opening/erosion as a generic cleanup without checking thin printable features; never use closing/dilation without checking unintended component merges and filled intentional counters/holes.
- Keep alpha-boundary geometry separate from edge RGB decontamination. A geometrically good matte can still produce a colored/bright fringe on a new garment background.
- Resampling algorithm and sharpening are separate provenance stages. Record resample kernel and scale first; record sharpening type/amount/radius/threshold separately, then run halo/ringing/noise-amplification QA.
- For deterministic enlargement, PSNR/SSIM can support regression testing when a reference exists, but DTF acceptance additionally needs edge displacement, minimum-stroke survival, text readability, alpha integrity, and artifact/halo checks.
