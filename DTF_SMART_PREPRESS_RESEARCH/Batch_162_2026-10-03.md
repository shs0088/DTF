## Synthesis update

### Background-removal routing
Add a deterministic branch for simple backgrounds:
border sampling -> perceptual/color-distance mask -> edge-connected flood fill -> manual/automatic restore -> alpha-edge refinement -> RGB edge decontamination -> composite QA.
Do not substitute global color knockout when the same color may legitimately occur inside the artwork.

### Halo diagnosis
Represent at least three independent failure classes:
1. source-alpha debris / accidental fractional alpha;
2. underbase geometry error (choke/spread/threshold);
3. production registration / mechanical drift.
A single generic "white halo" flag is insufficient.

### Alpha policy
Hard thresholding is an optional repair for accidental haze, not a universal DTF rule. Preserve intentional fractional transparency until classified. Re-run topology, thin-feature, and representative-garment composite QA after any alpha clipping.

### Resolution provenance
Evaluate effective PPI at final physical size and preserve resize history. Enlargement cannot create missing source detail, and resampling may introduce new fractional-alpha edge pixels.
