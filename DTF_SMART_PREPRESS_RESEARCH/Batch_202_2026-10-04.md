## Accepted pages

   - DTF white underbase/RIP behavior.
   - Distinguishes RIP-generated white from an explicit per-pixel W1 spot channel.
   - Semi-transparent artwork can require proportional white rather than a binary alpha>0 underbase.
   - Named spot-channel metadata is operationally important for automated RIP ingestion.
   - Choke is registration compensation but excessive choke destroys thin supported features.
   - Halftoned white is an output-condition operation and should be validated at final size.

2. https://bytetools.bytevancer.com/image-alpha-channel-extractor

## Engineering synthesis delta

### White-underbase representation
Add explicit fields:
- white_source_mode = rip_generated | explicit_spot_channel
- white_channel_name
- white_support_function
- white_density_map
- choke_physical_mm
- choke_pixel_equivalent_at_output_dpi
- white_feature_survival_after_choke
- rip_spot_channel_mapping_verified

Do not infer that every alpha>0 pixel deserves full white. Fractional-alpha fades, smoke, glass, soft shadows and antialiasing require a white-support policy that is separate from the artwork alpha.

### Alpha QA
Add:
- alpha_zero_fraction
- alpha_fractional_fraction
- alpha_opaque_fraction
- alpha_min
- alpha_max
- alpha_threshold_applied
- alpha_threshold_value
- threshold_topology_delta
- threshold_thin_feature_loss

A PNG format check is insufficient: an opaque PNG can contain no useful transparency at all. Continuous alpha must not be binarized as generic halo cleanup without semantic/topology checks.

### Halftone/output-condition QA
Keep halftone generation downstream of final physical sizing. Record:
- screen_dot_size
- screen_angle
- screen_density_or_lpi
- output_printer_profile
- film_profile
- final_physical_size
- post_screening_resize_detected

Any resize after screening invalidates screening QA. No universal LPI should be hard-coded from vendor guidance; calibration/test transfers remain authoritative.
